## Independent assessment: Unseen business brief

Respond to an unfamiliar business case with an evidence-backed plan, sample creatives, a measurement approach and a risk register, then defend it to an independent assessor who will challenge your assumptions and ask for revisions.

You'll receive the brief at the start of the assessment window. It is a case you haven't seen before. You may use your notes and an approved AI assistant, and you must log your AI use.

### What to submit

1. **Your growth plan.** Goal, segments, channels, objectives, KPIs and budget, with every figure traced to the brief or marked as an assumption.
2. **Your sample creatives.** At least two pieces of copy and one visual, with alt text and a short rationale.
3. **Your measurement approach.** How you'd track results and judge them without overstating attribution.
4. **Your risk register.** Each risk, its likelihood and impact, and your response.
5. **Your AI-use log.** Every use of AI during the assessment and how you checked it.

### How it's judged

An independent assessor scores your work against the pathway rubric. You need **80% overall** and a pass on every mandatory requirement: no fabricated claims, figures or testimonials; no unauthorised spend or communications; every figure traceable; privacy protected.

After the oral defence you'll receive a written decision: **competent** or **not yet competent**, with feedback. If it's "not yet", you'll get a remediation plan and a reassessment date.
