## Evidence: Partner business growth project

Help your partner business improve one clearly defined customer-acquisition or engagement process, with its approval at every step.

### What to submit

1. **Your partner brief.** The brief, signed off by the owner and your mentor.
2. **Your approved assets.** Each asset used, with the date and form of approval.
3. **Your implementation log.** Every action, who approved it, what happened, and any problems and fixes.
4. **Your handover notes.** Accounts touched, what's scheduled, what to watch and how your access was removed.

### What the assessor looks for

- A tightly defined scope
- Approval before every external action
- A complete, honest log
- A business able to continue without you

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
