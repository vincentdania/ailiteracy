## Baseline: Diagnose a small business's growth problem

Read the Zuma Bakes case in the baseline lesson and spend about 90 minutes writing a two-page growth diagnosis. Use only the facts in the case, and mark anything else as unknown.

The baseline is diagnostic. It isn't scored towards your certificate, and nobody is turned away because of it.

### What to submit

1. **Your diagnosis.** The two pages, under the five headings in the lesson: the goal, the customer, why the boosted posts didn't work, three ideas, and what you'd ask the owner.
2. **Your self-rating.** Your 1 to 4 rating for each of the five competencies, your strongest area and the area you most want to improve.
3. **Your AI use.** Whether you used an AI assistant, what for, and how you checked what it gave you.
