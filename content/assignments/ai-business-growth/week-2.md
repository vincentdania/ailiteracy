## Evidence: Competitor scan, segments and research evidence

Run proportionate research for your partner business: scan at least five competitors, hold at least three customer conversations, and define the segments that matter.

### What to submit

1. **Your competitor scan.** At least five competitors, with the date you checked each and three things the scan suggests.
2. **Your customer segments.** Two or three segments, each with needs, how they decide, objections and where to reach them.
3. **Your research evidence log.** Every key claim, where it came from, and how strong the evidence is. Note sample sizes.
4. **Your interview themes.** The themes from your conversations, with no names or contact details.

### What the assessor looks for

- Research sized to the decision
- Segments that would change how you market
- Honest ratings of evidence strength
- AI summaries checked against originals

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
