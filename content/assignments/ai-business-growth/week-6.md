## Evidence: Performance analysis and recommendations

Analyse your campaign's results (or the practice dataset your mentor provides) and recommend what to do next.

### What to submit

1. **Your measurement dashboard.** A link to your Google Sheet, with the sources and working formulas visible.
2. **Your performance analysis.** What the data shows, what it might mean and what it can't show, with counts beside percentages.
3. **Your recommendations.** Each recommendation with its evidence, your confidence and a test to check it.

### What the assessor looks for

- Correct calculations
- Platform figures reconciled with the business's own records
- No overstated attribution or causal claims
- Recommendations that follow from the evidence

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
