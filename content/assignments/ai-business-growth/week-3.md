## Evidence: Revised copy, social assets and creative rationale

Create content for one segment and one action. Use AI to draft variations, then edit for accuracy, tone and accessibility.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised copy.** At least three pieces, each shown before and after.
2. **Your social assets.** At least two graphics or short videos, with alt text, as links or images.
3. **Your creative rationale.** How each choice links to the segment, the action and your week 2 research.
4. **Your claims check.** Every factual claim in your content and its source. Confirm you have permission for every photo.

### What the assessor looks for

- Accurate, truthful claims only
- A clear audience and one call to action
- Accessible design and alt text
- Choices justified by evidence

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
