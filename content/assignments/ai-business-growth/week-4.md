## Evidence: Campaign plan and creative brief

Plan one campaign for your partner business, from objective to budget, and agree it with the owner.

### What to submit

1. **Your channel plan.** The channels you'll use and the research behind each choice.
2. **Your objectives and KPIs.** SMART objectives and the KPIs for each funnel stage, with the primary KPI marked.
3. **Your budget.** Each line, the stop rule, and the owner's written approval (name, date and how they approved).
4. **Your creative brief.** The completed brief for the main campaign asset.

### What the assessor looks for

- Objectives tied to the business goal
- Channels chosen from evidence
- KPIs that measure behaviour, not vanity
- Client-controlled spend with a stop rule

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
