## Evidence: Supervised campaign configuration and tracking plan

Configure your campaign in a sandbox, or as a draft in the business's own account, and build the tracking that will let you measure it honestly.

### What to submit

1. **Your campaign configuration.** Screenshots or an export of the settings, with account IDs, emails and phone numbers hidden.
2. **Your tracking plan.** How each KPI is counted, where, by whom and how often, including UTM-tagged links.
3. **Your lead capture design.** What personal data you collect, why each field is needed, and how consent is given.
4. **Your pre-launch check.** The completed checklist with the owner's approval, recorded before launch.

### What the assessor looks for

- Settings that match the approved plan
- Minimal personal data and no sensitive targeting
- Tracking that would actually work
- Clear client control of account and budget

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
