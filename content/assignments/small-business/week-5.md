## Build assignment: Your week in one message

Run your weekly report assistant and check that every number in it is exact.

### What to submit

1. **Your report.** Paste the weekly report your assistant sent to Telegram.
2. **Your totals.** The total from **Add up the week** in n8n, and the total from your own records. Do they match? If not, say why.
3. **Your schedule.** The day and time your report runs, and the **Timezone** in your workflow settings.
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Read your numbers

Look at your report as a business owner, not a builder.

What does it tell you about your week? What sold best, and does that surprise you? Read the AI's idea for next week: would you follow it? Explain why or why not. Finally, name one more number you'd like in your report, and how it would help you decide something.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** What your week tells you, your view of the AI's idea, and the number you'd add.
