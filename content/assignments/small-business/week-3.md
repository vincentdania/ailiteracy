## Build assignment: Answer a customer, with your approval

Connect WhatsApp, then build your customer reply assistant and test it the way a customer would: once with a question it can answer, and once with a question it can't.

### What to submit

1. **Your System Message.** Paste the System Message from your AI step, with your real prices, areas and hours. Replace any phone number in it with X's.
2. **Your approved test.** The question you sent from your phone, the draft that arrived on Telegram, and the reply that arrived on WhatsApp after you tapped **Approve**.
3. **Your declined test.** A question your facts don't answer, the draft it wrote, and what Telegram said after you tapped **Decline**.
4. **Published or not.** Is your workflow published? If not yet, say what's stopping you, for example Meta's checks, and what you've done about it.
5. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Your customers' top questions

List the five questions your customers ask most. For each one, say whether your assistant can answer it from your System Message, or whether it should say a person will reply.

Then pick the one question where a wrong answer from the AI would cost you money or a customer's trust. Explain how your System Message handles it now, and what you'd change.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your five questions, who answers each one, and your riskiest question.
