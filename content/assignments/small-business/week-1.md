## Build assignment: Your agent card and safety check

Plan the first assistant you'll build for your business, then check the plan is safe before you build anything. You'll use this card again in week 3.

### What to submit

1. **Your agent card.** Paste your whole card from the "How AI agents work" lesson, with all five parts filled in: role, tools, memory, trigger and approval. No square brackets left.
2. **Your safety checklist.** Paste the ten-point checklist from the "Safe access and approvals" lesson, with yes or no on every line. Lines 9 and 10 can say "not yet".
3. **What you changed.** One thing the checklist made you change on your card, and why. If nothing changed, say which line was hardest to answer yes to, and why.

## Apply assignment: Where AI fits in your business

Think about a normal week in your business.

Describe your business in two sentences. Then list the three jobs that take up most of your week. Pick the one you'd hand to an assistant first, and explain why it's a good first job. Finally, name one job you would never hand to AI, and say why. The safety rules about money, refunds and promises to customers may help.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your business, your three jobs, your first assistant, and the job you'd never hand over.
