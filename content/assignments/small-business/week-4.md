## Build assignment: Follow up three leads

Run your lead follow-up assistant on three test leads and check that each one is ready to send, with one tap, to the right person.

### What to submit

1. **Your three Telegram messages.** Paste the three follow-ups your assistant sent you. Change the customers' names and replace phone numbers with X's.
2. **Your Follow_up dates.** For each lead, the **Follow_up** date before you ran the workflow and after. Each should move three days ahead.
3. **Your time zone.** The **Timezone** you set in your workflow settings.
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Your follow-up rules

Most businesses get the same few kinds of lead again and again. For example, someone asking for a price, someone planning an event, or someone who went quiet after a quote.

Write follow-up rules for your three most common kinds of lead. For each one, say how soon to follow up, how often, what the message should say, and when to stop. Explain why each rule fits your customers.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your three kinds of lead, your rules for each, and why.
