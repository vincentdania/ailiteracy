## Portfolio project: Your AI team, on show

This replaces an exam. You'll publish a page about the assistants you built, as a short case study a customer, partner or employer can read. It stays online after the course, and your certificate links to it.

Follow the "Publish your portfolio on GitHub Pages" lesson. It takes you from a new GitHub account to a live page, step by step.

### Your page must include

1. **The problem.** What took up your time, or what was being missed, before your assistants.
2. **What your assistants do.** Each assistant in one or two sentences. Feature at least one in more detail.
3. **How it works.** The steps of your featured assistant, from trigger to result, and where you approve before anything reaches a customer.
4. **Sample results.** At least two real examples, such as a reply, a brief or a report, with names and numbers changed. Screenshots are welcome.
5. **Safety rules.** How your assistants keep customers' information safe, and how you'd stop them.
6. **What's next.** What you'd improve or build next.

### Your repository must include

1. **A README** that says what the repository is, in a few lines.
2. **Your workflows.** At least your featured workflow, downloaded from n8n (open it, click the **...** menu at the top right, then **Download**). Before you upload it, open the file and replace your Telegram ID and any phone numbers with X's.

### Leave out

Never publish a password, an API key, a bot token, an app secret, a customer's name or a phone number. The site scans your page for keys, tokens and phone numbers and sends it back if it finds any.

### What to submit

1. **Your page.** The link to your GitHub Pages site, starting `https://` and ending `github.io/...`
2. **Your repository.** The link to your GitHub repository, starting `https://github.com/`
3. **Your one-line summary.** One sentence about your project, to show beside your certificate.
