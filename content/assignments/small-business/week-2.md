## Build assignment: n8n and Telegram, working together

Show that your n8n workshop runs and that it can reach you on Telegram and wait for your answer.

> **Start week 3 now.** Next week you connect WhatsApp through Meta. Meta's account and business checks can take time, so create your business portfolio and your Meta developer account this week, following Parts 1 and 2 of the WhatsApp lesson. Week 3's deadline is hard.

### What to submit

1. **Your first workflow.** Paste the message your **My first workflow** produced when both boxes turned green.
2. **Your Telegram test.** Paste the message your **Telegram test** workflow sent to your phone, and say what happened when you tapped **Approve**.
3. **Your safety setup.** Say which of these are on: 2FA on your n8n account, a screen lock on your phone, and two-step verification in Telegram. Never paste a password, code or bot token.
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it. If everything worked, say which step took you longest and why.

## Apply assignment: Your off switches

It's 9pm. A customer tells you your assistant sent them the wrong price.

Walk through exactly what you'd do in the first ten minutes. How would you stop the assistant? What would you say to the customer? How would you find and fix what went wrong? What one rule would you add so it can't happen again? Also say where you keep your bot token, and why it should never go in a chat, an email or a shared document.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your first ten minutes, your fix, your new rule, and where your token lives.
