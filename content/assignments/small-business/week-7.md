## Build assignment: Remind a late payer

Send yourself a test payment reminder and check the invoice was updated.

### What to submit

1. **Your reminder.** Paste the reminder your assistant sent to Telegram. Change the customer's name and replace phone numbers with X's.
2. **Your updated invoice.** The test row in your **Invoices** table after the run: **Invoice**, **Amount**, **Due**, **Status** and **Last_reminder**. **Last_reminder** should show today's date.
3. **The WhatsApp button.** Say what happened when you tapped **Open in WhatsApp**. Did it open the right chat, with the message typed in?
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Your policy for late payers

Draft your policy for late payers. Cover the tone of your reminders, when the first one goes out, how often you send more, and the point where you stop the AI and call the customer yourself. Explain how your policy protects both your money and your relationship with the customer.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your tone, your timing, when you take over, and why.
