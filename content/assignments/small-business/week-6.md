## Build assignment: Start the day knowing what needs you

Run your daily brief and check it shows only what's due today.

### What to submit

1. **Your brief.** Paste the brief your assistant sent to Telegram. Change customers' names.
2. **Your test orders.** List the rows in your **Orders** table when you ran it, with the **Due**, **Time** and **Item** for each.
3. **What it left out.** Name one order or lead that correctly didn't appear in the brief, and why it was left out.
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Your morning routine

Describe how your mornings work today: how you find out what's due, and what gets missed. Then explain how the 7am brief will change that. Say what you'd do if the brief hadn't arrived by 7:15am, and name one thing you'd add to your brief, and why.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your mornings now, with the brief, when it fails, and what you'd add.
