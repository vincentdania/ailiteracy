## Build assignment: Your AI team, launched

Test all five assistants from start to end, and make sure you hear about it when one breaks.

### What to submit

1. **Your team.** List your five assistants by name, and say whether each one is **Published**.
2. **Your start-to-end tests.** One line for each assistant: what you sent or ran, and what came out. Change customers' names.
3. **Your test alert.** Paste the alert your **Tell me if something breaks** workflow sent to Telegram.
4. **Your weekly check.** The day and time of the weekly check in your calendar.
5. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Plan your portfolio

Next week you'll publish a portfolio page about the assistants you've built, for customers or employers to see. Plan it now.

What problem in your business did your assistants solve? Which one or two will you feature, and why? What results can you show, such as a time saved, a lead won or a payment collected? Which details must you leave out, such as customers' names, phone numbers and keys? What would you improve next?

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your problem, your featured assistants, your results, what you'll leave out, and what's next.
