## Evidence: Partner-approved functional pilot and handover

Run a partner-approved pilot on a real low-risk process, measure it honestly, and hand it over.

### What to submit

1. **Your pilot plan.** The signed plan and completed go-live checklist.
2. **Your pilot evidence.** The log, issues and how you responded.
3. **Your time and cost study.** Before and after times, including review and fixing time, and the monthly running costs.
4. **Your handover notes.** The notes, and evidence that someone else followed them without your help.

### What the assessor looks for

- A safe, approved pilot
- Human approval kept for external communications
- Honest measurement including costs
- A business able to run it without you

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
