## Baseline: Map and improve a messy process

Read the Kubwa Print Hub case in the baseline lesson and spend about 90 minutes writing a two-page process diagnosis. Use only the facts in the case, and mark anything else as unknown.

The baseline is diagnostic. It isn't scored towards your certificate, and nobody is turned away because of it.

### What to submit

1. **Your diagnosis.** The two pages, under the five headings in the lesson: the process as it is, the pain points, what to simplify first, one automation, and what could go wrong.
2. **Your self-rating.** Your 1 to 4 rating for each of the five competencies, your strongest area and the area you most want to improve.
3. **Your AI use.** Whether you used an AI assistant, what for, and how you checked what it gave you.
