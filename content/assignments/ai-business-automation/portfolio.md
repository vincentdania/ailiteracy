## Graduate portfolio

Your portfolio is the lasting evidence of your competence. It collects the final, revised version of the work you produced across the pathway, so a future employer or client can see what you can do.

### Your portfolio must include

1. **As-is and to-be process maps.** From weeks 1 and 2, as revised after feedback.
2. **Working automation.** Your best workflow, exported without credentials, with a short description.
3. **Test log.** Your week 6 test log.
4. **Risk assessment.** Your week 6 risk assessment.
5. **Instructions.** Your week 7 handover notes.
6. **Before and after time study.** Your measured times and operating costs.

### Leave out

Never include a password, an API key, an account number, or any person's name, phone number, email or personal details. Use a code name for the partner business unless they've agreed in writing to be named, and get their permission before publishing any work sample.

### What to submit

1. **Your portfolio.** A link to a shared folder or a published page with every item above.
2. **Your permissions.** For each work sample from a partner, who gave permission to include it and how.
3. **Your one-line summary.** One sentence about your strongest piece of work, to show beside your certificate.
