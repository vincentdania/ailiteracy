## Evidence: Test cases, failure handling, access and privacy checks

Test your prototype on a bad day: bad data, duplicates, volume, service failures and security. Then review access and privacy.

### What to submit

1. **Your test log.** At least 12 test cases across every type, with failures, fixes and re-tests.
2. **Your failure handling.** Your error workflow, retries and alerts, with evidence that a failure was caught.
3. **Your access and privacy review.** Every person, account and credential with access, what was removed, and where personal data flows.
4. **Your risk assessment.** Each risk with likelihood, impact, control and owner.

### What the assessor looks for

- Honest testing of failures
- Safe failure behaviour
- Least access and protected credentials
- A realistic risk assessment

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
