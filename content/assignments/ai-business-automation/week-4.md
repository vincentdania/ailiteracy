## Evidence: AI-assisted classification or drafting with review rules

Add an AI step for classification or drafting, limited to safe outputs, with review rules that keep people in control.

### What to submit

1. **Your AI-assisted workflow.** The exported workflow (no credentials) and the system prompt.
2. **Your review rules.** The table of what passes automatically and what goes to a person.
3. **Your AI step test.** Results on at least 20 test inputs, including two prompt-injection attempts, with failures reported honestly.
4. **Your AI decision log.** A sample of logged AI decisions and reviews.

### What the assessor looks for

- AI limited to labels or drafts
- Validated outputs with unexpected results caught
- Risky cases routed to people
- Honest test results

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
