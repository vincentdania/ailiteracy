## Evidence: As-is process map and pain-point inventory

Observe one process at your partner business (or use the Kubwa Print Hub case), map it as it really happens, and list what goes wrong.

### What to submit

1. **Your as-is process map.** A swimlane map with every step, handoff and data store, as an image or link.
2. **Your pain-point inventory.** Each pain point with frequency, time or cost, risk, and whether to automate, simplify or leave it.
3. **Your observation notes.** When and how you observed, and your timings. No personal data.

### What the assessor looks for

- A map based on observation, not assumption
- Measurements rather than guesses
- Good judgement about what not to automate

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
