## Evidence: To-be workflow, data dictionary and integration plan

Design the improved process and the data that runs through it, before you build anything.

### What to submit

1. **Your to-be workflow.** The improved process, with each step marked manual, automated or review gate, and what changed from the as-is.
2. **Your data dictionary.** Every field in every sheet the automation touches: meaning, type, format, allowed values, required, and who updates it.
3. **Your integration plan.** Each trigger, action, data moved, account used and the smallest permission it needs.
4. **User feedback.** What the person who will use the process said about the design, and what you changed.

### What the assessor looks for

- Waste removed before automating
- Unambiguous data rules and unique IDs
- Least access for every connection
- Review gates before external actions

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
