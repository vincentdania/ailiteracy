## Evidence: Working trigger-action workflow with logs

Build a working workflow in n8n or Make against test copies of your sheets, with an audit log for every run.

### What to submit

1. **Your workflow.** The exported workflow file with credentials removed, and a step-by-step description.
2. **Your audit log.** A sample showing successful runs, a skipped duplicate and at least one failure you fixed.
3. **Your build notes.** Field mappings, how duplicates are prevented, and what you fixed.

### What the assessor looks for

- Correct mapping to the data dictionary
- Steps that are safe to repeat
- A useful audit log
- Evidence you can diagnose failures

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
