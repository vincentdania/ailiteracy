## Evidence: Reminder, reporting or invoicing prototype

Build a complete prototype for one business use case on test data, and measure the manual process before it changes.

### What to submit

1. **Your prototype.** The exported workflow (no credentials) and a one-page description.
2. **Your edge cases.** How it handles zero results, many results, missing data and errors.
3. **Your review gate.** Where a person approves and the log showing approvals or skips.
4. **Your before time study.** Measured times for the manual process.

### What the assessor looks for

- A complete, working prototype
- Safe edge-case handling
- A real review gate before anything external
- Measured baseline times

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
