## Independent assessment: Unseen workflow brief

Build and demonstrate an automation for an unfamiliar process, recover from a failure the assessor simulates, and explain your controls, data flow, permissions and handover.

You'll receive the brief at the start of the assessment window. It is a case you haven't seen before. You may use your notes and an approved AI assistant, and you must log your AI use.

### What to submit

1. **Your design.** To-be workflow, data dictionary and integration plan.
2. **Your automation.** The exported workflow with credentials removed, and its audit log.
3. **Your test log.** Success and failure scenarios, including the simulated failure and how you recovered.
4. **Your handover summary.** How to run it, check it, switch it off and remove access.
5. **Your AI-use log.** Every use of AI during the assessment and how you checked it.

### How it's judged

An independent assessor scores your work against the pathway rubric. You need **80% overall** and a pass on every mandatory requirement: no external messages or payments without human approval; no data corruption; least access; no personal data sent to unapproved services; no exposed credentials.

After the oral defence you'll receive a written decision: **competent** or **not yet competent**, with feedback. If it's "not yet", you'll get a remediation plan and a reassessment date.
