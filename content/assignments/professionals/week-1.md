## Build assignment: Your agent card and safety check

Plan the first assistant you'll build for your work, then check the plan is safe before you build anything. You'll come back to this card as you build.

### What to submit

1. **Your agent card.** Paste your whole card from the "How AI agents work" lesson, with all five parts filled in: role, tools, memory, trigger and approval. No square brackets left.
2. **Your safety checklist.** Paste the ten-point checklist from the "Safe access and approvals" lesson, with yes or no on every line. Lines 9 and 10 can say "not yet".
3. **What you changed.** One thing the checklist made you change on your card, and why. If nothing changed, say which line was hardest to answer yes to, and why.

## Apply assignment: Where AI fits in your work

Think about a normal working week.

Describe your work in two sentences. Then list the three tasks that take up most of your week. Pick the one you'd hand to an assistant first, and explain why it's a good first task. Finally, name one task you would never hand to AI, and say why. Think about client confidentiality, judgement calls and anything with your name on it.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your work, your three tasks, your first assistant, and the task you'd never hand over.
