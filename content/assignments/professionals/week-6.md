## Build assignment: Write in your own voice

Teach Hermes how you write, draft a proposal from your own facts, and send a client update you've checked.

### What to submit

1. **Your style rules.** Paste the description of your writing style that Hermes saved.
2. **Your proposal draft.** Paste one section of the proposal Hermes drafted, before you filled it in, with every **[CHECK]** still showing. Hide names and prices.
3. **Your client update.** Paste the update as you sent it from Gmail, with names hidden.
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Your voice

What makes your writing sound like you? Give three examples from your style rules, and three words or phrases you'd ban from your emails.

Then explain where AI drafting is risky in your work, such as prices, deadlines or promises to clients. Write the rule you'll follow before anything Hermes drafts goes out.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your voice, your banned words, your risks and your rule.
