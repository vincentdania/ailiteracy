## Build assignment: Ask your board, then lock it down

Ask your board a real question, then run the safety check that keeps your clients' information protected.

### What to submit

1. **Your question.** The question you sent, starting with `Board:`. Hide names.
2. **Your board's answer.** Paste the answer, or at least where the advisers agreed and disagreed.
3. **Your follow-up.** The follow-up question you asked one adviser, and what it said, in a few lines.
4. **Your decision.** What you decided, and whether the board changed your mind.
5. **Your safety check.** Say whether **Redact Secrets** is on, whether only your user ID is allowed in Telegram, and the date of your monthly safety check.

## Apply assignment: Your traffic-light list

Write your traffic-light list. **Green:** what you'll share with Hermes freely. **Amber:** what you'll share with care, using roles instead of names. **Red:** what you'll never share. Give at least three examples of each from your own work, and explain where you drew the line between amber and red, and why.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your green, amber and red lists, and where you drew the line.
