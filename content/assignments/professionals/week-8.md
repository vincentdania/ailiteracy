## Build assignment: Your assistant, launched

Test every job your assistant does, and add your weekly review.

### What to submit

1. **Your tests.** One line for each job: inbox triage, morning briefing, meeting follow-ups, proposals and updates, mentor board, and a request from your phone. Say what you sent and what came out.
2. **Your weekly review.** Paste the review that arrived on Telegram when you clicked **Trigger now**. Replace names with roles.
3. **Acting on it.** Paste the full request you sent to act on one thing in your review.
4. **Your monthly safety check.** The date it's in your calendar.
5. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Plan your portfolio

Next week you'll publish a portfolio page about the assistant you've built, for clients or employers to see. Plan it now.

What problem in your work did your assistant solve? Which one or two jobs will you feature, and why? What results can you show, such as hours saved each week or a follow-up that didn't get dropped? Which details must you leave out, such as client names, email addresses and tokens? What would you improve next?

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your problem, your featured jobs, your results, what you'll leave out, and what's next.
