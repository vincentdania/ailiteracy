## Build assignment: From meeting notes to a sent follow-up

Turn the notes from a real meeting into decisions, actions and a follow-up email, and check it all against what really happened.

### What to submit

1. **Your action list.** Paste the decisions, action items and open questions Hermes produced. Replace names with roles, and hide company names.
2. **Your corrections.** What you changed in the list to match what really happened, and why. If nothing needed changing, say how you checked.
3. **Your follow-up email.** Paste the email as you sent it from Gmail, with names hidden.
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Your meetings

Which of your meetings create the most follow-up work, and what usually gets dropped? Explain how you'll use Hermes after those meetings, and whether you'll paste notes or send voice notes.

Then write your rule for what never goes into notes you give Hermes, and why.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your meetings, how you'll use Hermes after them, and your rule.
