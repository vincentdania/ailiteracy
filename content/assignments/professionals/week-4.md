## Build assignment: A briefing on your phone every morning

Schedule your morning briefing and test it straight away.

### What to submit

1. **Your briefing.** Paste the briefing that arrived on Telegram when you clicked **Trigger now**. Replace names with roles.
2. **Your schedule.** The days and time your briefing runs, and the next run that **Scheduled jobs** shows.
3. **Your check.** Compare the briefing with your real calendar. Name one thing it got right, and anything it got wrong or missed.
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Your morning routine

Describe how your working day starts today: how you find out what's coming, and what gets missed. Then explain how the briefing will change that.

Your briefing only runs when your computer is on and Hermes is open. Say how you'll make sure that happens, and what you'd do on a morning it doesn't arrive. Finally, name one thing you'd add to your briefing, and why.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your mornings now, with the briefing, when it fails, and what you'd add.
