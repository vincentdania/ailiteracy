## Build assignment: Hermes, on your computer and your phone

Show that Hermes answers you, asks before it acts, and can reach you on Telegram.

### What to submit

1. **Your first reply.** Paste the reply Hermes gave to your first message.
2. **Your approvals.** Say what **Settings**, **Safety**, **Approvals** shows for Approval Mode. It should be **Manual**.
3. **Your Telegram test.** Paste the reply Hermes sent to your phone, and say what happened when you sent `/pause` and then `/pause off`.
4. **Your safety setup.** Say whether your phone has a screen lock and Telegram has two-step verification. Never paste a password, code or bot token.
5. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it. If everything worked, say which step took you longest and why.

## Apply assignment: Your off switches

You're in a client meeting. Your phone shows Hermes asking to do something you didn't expect.

Walk through what you'd do, step by step. When would you reject, and when would you send `/pause`? How would you find out what went wrong afterwards? Name three actions Hermes must always ask you about before it does them, and explain why. Also say where your bot token lives, and why it should never go in a chat, an email or a shared document.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** What you'd do, your three "always ask" actions, and where your token lives.
