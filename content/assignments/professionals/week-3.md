## Build assignment: Triage your real inbox

Connect Gmail, triage your last 24 hours of email, and send one reply yourself from a draft Hermes saved.

### What to submit

1. **Your piles.** Paste the triage Hermes gave you. Replace people's names with roles, like "client" or "my manager", and hide company names.
2. **One draft.** Paste one draft reply Hermes saved to your Gmail **Drafts**, with names hidden. Say what you changed before you sent it.
3. **Your rules.** Paste the triage rules Hermes remembered.
4. **What went wrong.** One thing that didn't work the first time, and how you fixed it.

## Apply assignment: Your inbox rules

List five rules for your inbox that Hermes should follow. For example, who always counts as urgent, what can wait, and how your replies should sound. Explain why each rule matters in your work.

Then name one kind of email you'd never let Hermes draft, even for you to check, and say why.

### What to submit

1. **Your answer (250 to 400 words).** Your five rules, why each matters, and the email Hermes never drafts.
