## Portfolio project: Your AI assistant, on show

This replaces an exam. You'll publish a page about the assistant you built, as a short case study a client or employer can read. It stays online after the course, and your certificate links to it.

Follow the "Publish your portfolio on GitHub Pages" lesson. It takes you from a new GitHub account to a live page, step by step.

### Your page must include

1. **The problem.** What took up your time, or what was being dropped, before your assistant.
2. **What your assistant does.** Each job in one or two sentences. Feature at least one in more detail.
3. **How it works.** The steps of your featured job, from your request or schedule to the result, and where you approve or press Send yourself.
4. **Sample results.** At least two real examples, such as a briefing, a triage or a weekly review, with names and details changed. Screenshots are welcome.
5. **Safety rules.** Your traffic-light rule, your approvals, and how you'd stop your assistant.
6. **What's next.** What you'd improve or build next.

### Your repository must include

1. **A README** that says what the repository is, in a few lines.
2. **Your assistant's setup.** A file called `my-assistant.md` with the requests you use, your scheduled jobs and their schedules, and the rules Hermes follows, such as your triage rules and writing style.

### Leave out

Never publish a password, an API key, a bot token, a client's name, an email address or a phone number. The site scans your page for keys, tokens and phone numbers and sends it back if it finds any.

### What to submit

1. **Your page.** The link to your GitHub Pages site, starting `https://` and ending `github.io/...`
2. **Your repository.** The link to your GitHub repository, starting `https://github.com/`
3. **Your one-line summary.** One sentence about your project, to show beside your certificate.
