## Independent assessment: Unseen project recovery case

Diagnose a project in trouble that you haven't seen before, produce a recovery and delivery plan, and defend your planning, cost and resource logic, risk responses, stakeholder decisions and AI verification to an independent assessor.

You'll receive the brief at the start of the assessment window. It is a case you haven't seen before. You may use your notes and an approved AI assistant, and you must log your AI use.

### What to submit

1. **Your diagnosis.** What has gone wrong and why, with evidence from the case.
2. **Your recovery and delivery plan.** Revised scope if needed, schedule logic, budget and resources.
3. **Your risk register and communication plan.** Updated for the recovery.
4. **Your status report and change analysis.** With options and a recommendation, and who decides.
5. **Your AI review log.** Every AI-assisted output, what you checked and corrected.

### How it's judged

An independent assessor scores your work against the pathway rubric. You need **80% overall** and a pass on every mandatory requirement: honest status reporting; decisions beyond your authority escalated; no fabricated figures or progress; safety and safeguarding risks addressed; AI outputs verified.

After the oral defence you'll receive a written decision: **competent** or **not yet competent**, with feedback. If it's "not yet", you'll get a remediation plan and a reassessment date.
