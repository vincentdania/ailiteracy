## Evidence: Supervised project phase

Coordinate a supervised phase of your partner project, handling the disruption your mentor injects.

### What to submit

1. **Your coordination evidence.** Daily check-in notes and communications sent, with personal data removed.
2. **Your decision and issue logs.** Kept current through the week, with authority for every decision.
3. **Your change log.** Including how you handled the injected disruption.
4. **Your status report and lessons learned.** The week's status report and your lessons-learned table.

### What the assessor looks for

- Steady, visible coordination
- Decisions made at the right level
- Issues and changes handled formally
- Honest reporting and useful lessons

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
