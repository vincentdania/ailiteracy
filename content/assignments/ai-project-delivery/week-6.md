## Evidence: Status report, change analysis and recovery options

Respond to the disruption scenario your mentor gives you: report status honestly, analyse the change, and offer recovery options.

### What to submit

1. **Your status report.** RAG ratings with reasons and evidence, milestones, issues and decisions needed.
2. **Your change analysis.** At least three options, each with its impact on scope, time, cost, quality and risk.
3. **Your recovery recommendation.** Your recommended option, why, and the escalation route and message.

### What the assessor looks for

- Honest status backed by evidence
- Complete impact analysis
- Specific options with trade-offs
- Decisions escalated to the right authority

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
