## Evidence: Delivery approach, backlog and AI review log

Choose a predictive, agile or hybrid approach for your project with reasons, set up a backlog, and record how you've verified AI outputs.

### What to submit

1. **Your delivery approach.** The approach you chose and why, using the four factors in the lesson.
2. **Your backlog.** At least ten ordered user stories with acceptance criteria.
3. **Your AI review log.** Each AI-assisted planning output, what you checked and changed, and who approved it.

### What the assessor looks for

- An approach justified by uncertainty and constraints
- Fixed commitments kept under control
- A backlog ordered by value
- Honest verification of AI outputs

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
