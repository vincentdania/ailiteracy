## Evidence: Scope statement, WBS and acceptance criteria

Define exactly what the project will deliver, break it into work packages and agree when each deliverable is done.

### What to submit

1. **Your scope statement.** Deliverables, exclusions, constraints and assumptions.
2. **Your WBS.** To work-package level, including project management.
3. **Your acceptance criteria.** For each major deliverable: what done means, how it's checked and who signs it off.

### What the assessor looks for

- Complete and bounded scope
- A WBS that follows the 100% rule
- Specific, checkable acceptance criteria

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
