## Graduate portfolio

Your portfolio is the lasting evidence of your competence. It collects the final, revised version of the work you produced across the pathway, so a future employer or client can see what you can do.

### Your portfolio must include

1. **Project charter.** From week 1, as revised.
2. **WBS.** From week 2.
3. **Schedule.** From week 3, with dependencies and critical path.
4. **Cost baseline.** Your week 3 budget.
5. **Risk register.** From week 4, updated through week 7.
6. **Communications plan.** From week 4.
7. **Progress report.** From weeks 6 and 7.
8. **Change log.** From week 7.
9. **Lessons learned.** From week 7.

### Leave out

Never include a password, an API key, an account number, or any person's name, phone number, email or personal details. Use a code name for the partner organisation unless they've agreed in writing to be named, and get their permission before publishing any work sample.

### What to submit

1. **Your portfolio.** A link to a shared folder or a published page with every item above.
2. **Your permissions.** For each work sample from a partner, who gave permission to include it and how.
3. **Your one-line summary.** One sentence about your strongest piece of work, to show beside your certificate.
