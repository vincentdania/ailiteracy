## Evidence: Logic-based schedule, budget and resource plan

Turn your WBS into a schedule driven by dependencies, a bottom-up budget and a resource plan, with the basis for every estimate.

### What to submit

1. **Your schedule.** Tasks, durations, dependencies, critical path and float, as a spreadsheet or Gantt export.
2. **Your budget.** Each line as units × rate or a quote, contingency linked to named risks, and the total against funding.
3. **Your resource plan.** Who is needed when, and how you resolved any overload.
4. **Your estimating basis.** The source of each major estimate, including at least two real quotes.

### What the assessor looks for

- Estimates with a stated basis
- Correct dependency logic and critical path
- A budget that adds up and is traceable
- A feasible resource plan

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
