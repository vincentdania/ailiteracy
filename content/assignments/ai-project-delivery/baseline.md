## Baseline: Rescue a one-page project plan

Read the Karu Digital Skills Week case in the baseline lesson and spend about 90 minutes writing a two-page plan review. Use only the facts in the case, and mark costs you'd need to confirm as "Quote needed".

The baseline is diagnostic. It isn't scored towards your certificate, and nobody is turned away because of it.

### What to submit

1. **Your plan review.** The two pages, under the five headings in the lesson: what's unclear, what's unrealistic, the top five risks, your improved outline, and who decides what.
2. **Your self-rating.** Your 1 to 4 rating for each of the five competencies, your strongest area and the area you most want to improve.
3. **Your AI use.** Whether you used an AI assistant, what for, and how you checked what it gave you.
