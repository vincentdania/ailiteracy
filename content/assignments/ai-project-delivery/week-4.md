## Evidence: Risk register, quality criteria and communication plan

Plan for what could go wrong, what good looks like, and how every stakeholder will stay informed.

### What to submit

1. **Your risk register.** Cause-event-effect statements, likelihood, impact, response, owner and trigger.
2. **Your quality criteria.** Criteria and checks for each main deliverable, with when and by whom.
3. **Your communication plan.** For each stakeholder: what, how often, channel, from whom, and the action asked.

### What the assessor looks for

- Well-written risks with practical responses and owners
- Safety and safeguarding risks addressed
- Quality planned in
- Communication matched to each stakeholder

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
