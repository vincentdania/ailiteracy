## Evidence: Project charter, objectives and stakeholder register

Start your partner project (or the Karu case) properly: agree its purpose, objectives and authority, and analyse its stakeholders.

### What to submit

1. **Your project charter.** Purpose, SMART objectives, in and out of scope, milestones, budget, key risks, sponsor and approval.
2. **Your stakeholder register.** Each stakeholder's interest, influence, wants, concerns and engagement approach.
3. **Your authority note.** What you can decide alone, and what you must escalate, to whom.

### What the assessor looks for

- SMART objectives tied to the need
- Clear scope boundaries
- Explicit authority and escalation
- A realistic stakeholder analysis

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
