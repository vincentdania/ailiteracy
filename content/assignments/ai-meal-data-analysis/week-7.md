## Evidence: Analysis package for a realistic NGO scenario

Produce the complete analysis package for the scenario your mentor assigns, answering the stakeholder's questions.

### What to submit

1. **Your analysis package.** All nine parts listed in the lesson, as files or links.
2. **Your stakeholder briefing.** Five minutes of slides or a one-page note for the programme manager.
3. **Your limitations statement.** Data and design limits, how they may affect the findings, and what would strengthen the evidence.

### What the assessor looks for

- Findings that answer the questions asked
- Outputs, outcomes and causal claims clearly separated
- Figures that reconcile everywhere
- An honest limitations statement

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
