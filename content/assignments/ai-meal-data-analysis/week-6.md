## Evidence: Validated findings, AI-use log and qualitative coding

Use AI responsibly to code qualitative feedback and draft findings, then validate everything against the data.

### What to submit

1. **Your validated findings.** A one-page summary where every finding links to a number or coded evidence.
2. **Your qualitative coding sample.** Your codebook, the coded responses and your validation results, including the agreement rate.
3. **Your AI-use log.** Every AI use this week: tool (and whether approved), input, what you kept and changed, and how you checked.

### What the assessor looks for

- De-identified data and approved tools only
- A human-checked codebook
- Honest agreement figures
- Findings and quotes that match the data exactly

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
