## Evidence: Theory of Change, results matrix and indicator reference sheets

Set out how the Rise project (or your partner project) is meant to create change, and define the indicators that will show whether it does.

### What to submit

1. **Your Theory of Change.** The if-then-because pathway from activities to outcomes, with assumptions marked and the riskiest identified.
2. **Your results matrix.** Activities, outputs, outcomes and their indicators, with baselines and targets where known.
3. **Your indicator reference sheets.** At least three, each with definition, numerator, denominator, source, frequency, disaggregation and limitations. Include one on participant feedback.

### What the assessor looks for

- Outputs and outcomes clearly distinguished
- Realistic, explicit assumptions
- Precise definitions and defensible denominators
- Accountability to participants built in

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
