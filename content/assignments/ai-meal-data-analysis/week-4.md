## Evidence: Indicator workbook and interpretation memo

Calculate your indicators from the clean data exactly as defined, and interpret them carefully.

### What to submit

1. **Your indicator workbook.** Formulas visible, with n and the denominator shown for every result, and a non-response sensitivity row.
2. **Your interpretation memo.** One page separating what the data shows from what it can't show.
3. **Your reconciliation check.** Totals that match across tables and the clean dataset.

### What the assessor looks for

- Calculations that follow the reference sheets
- Appropriate summaries (medians for income)
- Group sizes shown and small groups treated with care
- No causal claims without a suitable design

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
