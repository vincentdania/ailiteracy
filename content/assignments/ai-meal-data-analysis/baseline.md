## Baseline: Check a small programme dataset

Download the fictional Rise practice file from the baseline lesson and spend about 90 minutes writing a two-page data check. Don't upload any rows to an AI tool.

The baseline is diagnostic. It isn't scored towards your certificate, and nobody is turned away because of it.

### What to submit

1. **Your data check.** The two pages, under the five headings in the lesson: quality problems, your numbers, the donor claim, your rewrite, and what you'd need.
2. **Your calculations.** The share working at 6 months and the median incomes, each with its numerator and denominator.
3. **Your self-rating.** Your 1 to 4 rating for each of the five competencies, your strongest area and the area you most want to improve.
4. **Your AI use.** Whether you used an AI assistant, what for, and how you checked what it gave you.
