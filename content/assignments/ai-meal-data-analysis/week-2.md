## Evidence: KoboToolbox form, codebook and consent flow

Build a working KoboToolbox form that measures your indicators, protects respondents and produces analysis-ready data.

### What to submit

1. **Your form.** A link to your KoboToolbox form or the XLSForm file.
2. **Your codebook.** Every variable: name, question, type, values and codes, and skip logic.
3. **Your consent flow.** The consent text and what happens when someone refuses.
4. **Your test submissions.** Evidence that skip logic and constraints work, including a refusal and an out-of-range value.

### What the assessor looks for

- Every question mapped to an indicator
- Neutral, clear wording with time periods and units
- A real consent flow and minimal personal data
- Validation that works

## Revision: Act on your feedback

Your mentor will return written feedback on your evidence against the rubric. Revise your work, then explain what you changed. This is where most of the learning happens, so treat it as part of the week, not an extra.

### What to submit

1. **Your revised evidence.** The improved version, or a link to it, with the changed parts marked.
2. **What you changed.** Each feedback point, what you changed in response, and anything you chose not to change and why.
3. **AI-use log.** The rows you added this week: what you asked, what you kept, what you changed and how you checked it.
