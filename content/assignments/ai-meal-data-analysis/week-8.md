## Independent assessment: Unseen data challenge

Turn an unfamiliar programme dataset into reliable evidence: clean it, calculate the indicators, report and visualise the results, then defend your analysis to an independent assessor.

You'll receive the brief at the start of the assessment window. It is a case you haven't seen before. You may use your notes and an approved AI assistant, and you must log your AI use.

### What to submit

1. **Your cleaning log and clean dataset.** With reproducible steps from the raw file.
2. **Your indicator workbook.** Formulas visible, n shown for every result.
3. **Your report.** Two to four pages answering the stakeholder's questions, with a limitations statement.
4. **Your dashboard.** One to two pages, reconciled with the workbook.
5. **Your AI-use log.** Every use of AI during the assessment and how you checked it.

### How it's judged

An independent assessor scores your work against the pathway rubric. You need **80% overall** and a pass on every mandatory requirement: integrity (no invented, altered or selectively reported findings; every figure traceable) and privacy (no personal data exposed in outputs or to unapproved AI services).

After the oral defence you'll receive a written decision: **competent** or **not yet competent**, with feedback. If it's "not yet", you'll get a remediation plan and a reassessment date.
