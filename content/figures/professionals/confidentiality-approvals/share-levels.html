Before you paste anything

What can Hermes see?

Green · share freely

It's yours, or it's already public.

  • Your own notes, drafts and ideas
  • A client's public website or brochure
  • General questions about your work

Amber · share with care

Use roles, not names. Only what the task needs.

  • Client names, projects and prices
  • Meeting notes and email threads
  • Check the client's contract allows it

Red · never paste it

If it leaked, someone could be harmed.

  • Passwords, PINs and bank or card details
  • ID numbers, like NIN or BVN
  • Health records, and anything under an NDA
