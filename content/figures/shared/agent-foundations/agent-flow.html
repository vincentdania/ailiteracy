Example · Sunrise Bakery

How the five parts work together

1

Trigger

A customer sends a WhatsApp message.

Hi, do you deliver on Sundays?

2

Role + memory

It reads its job description and the bakery's delivery days.

3

Tools

It checks the delivery sheet in Google Sheets. Read only.

4

Draft

It writes a reply, but does not send it.

Hi Tunde, yes! We deliver on Sundays from 10am to 2pm.

5

Approval

Amaka, the owner, reads the draft on her phone.

ApproveEditReject

Edit or Reject: nothing is sent. Amaka fixes it or answers herself.

6

Sent

Only after Approve does the customer get the reply.
