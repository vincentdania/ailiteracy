Module 0 · Safe and responsible use

Five rules for every assistant

1

Smallest access that works

Only the apps its job needs. Read only where it only looks.

2

A person approves

Anything that reaches another person waits for your yes.

3

Protect private information

No passwords, card numbers, ID numbers or health details.

4

Be honest that it is AI

Tell people, and make it easy to reach a person.

5

Test, watch, keep an off switch

Test before launch, review weekly, know how to pause.
