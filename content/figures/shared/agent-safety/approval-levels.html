Rule 2 · A person approves

Three levels of approval

Green · it may act alone

Low risk, stays with you, easy to undo.

  • Sort emails into folders
  • Summarise notes or a report only you read

Amber · you approve each one

Anything that reaches another person.

  • Customer replies and follow-ups
  • Reminders and client updates
Every new assistant starts here

Red · a person always decides

It may prepare the facts, never the decision.

  • Money, refunds and discounts
  • Legal, medical or job decisions
  • Anything that cannot be undone
