**Time: about 2 hours · You'll need a document and a spreadsheet**

Before you learn anything new, you'll show what you can already do. The baseline task takes about 90 minutes and it isn't scored. It tells you and your mentor where to focus over the next eight weeks.

## What you'll have at the end

- A **baseline plan review** of a small project case
- A **self-rating** against the five competencies
- Your **strengths and gaps** written into your competence record

## The competency standard

| Competency | At the foundation standard, you can... |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Outcomes and scope | Translate a business or community need into clear outcomes and an authorised scope |
| 2. Estimates | Prepare a feasible schedule, budget and resource estimate, and show the logic behind it |
| 3. Control | Manage risks, issues, quality and stakeholder communication |
| 4. Adapt | Choose and adapt delivery methods (predictive, agile or hybrid) to fit the uncertainty and context |
| 5. Report and escalate | Report performance honestly and escalate decisions that are beyond your authority |

Your partner project: plan and coordinate a **supervised phase** of a small community digital-literacy or business improvement project. Scenario challenges will test how you handle disruptions and change control.

## What this pathway is not

Eight weeks builds **foundational project coordination**, not independent senior project-manager capability. It is not equivalent to PMP, PRINCE2 or any other third-party credential, and you must never describe it that way.

## The baseline case

Read this case. It's fictional.

> **Karu Digital Skills Week.** The chair of a community association in Karu, Nasarawa, wants to run free basic digital skills classes for 60 adults (smartphone use, WhatsApp Business, safe mobile banking) at the community hall. A bank has offered ₦1,800,000 if classes start within 10 weeks. The chair sends you this plan:
>
> "Week 1: get laptops. Week 2: find trainers. Week 3: advertise. Week 4 onwards: classes. Budget: laptops ₦1,500,000, trainers ₦300,000. My nephew will handle everything else. We'll see how it goes."
>
> Known: the hall has unreliable power; the bank wants a report on attendance and outcomes; most learners will bring their own phones. Unknown: trainer rates, how many people want to attend, whether the hall is free on weekdays.

## The baseline task

Spend about 90 minutes. Use an AI assistant if you like, and log how you used it.

Write a two-page plan review with these five headings:

1. **What's unclear.** What outcomes, scope and decisions are missing from the chair's plan?
2. **What's unrealistic.** Where are the schedule and budget not feasible, and why?
3. **The top five risks.** Each with likelihood, impact and a response.
4. **Your improved outline.** A one-page outline plan: outcomes, main deliverables, a rough schedule and budget headings.
5. **Who decides what.** Which decisions belong to the chair, the bank and the coordinator?

> **Stay safe:** Don't invent costs. Where you'd need a quote, write "Quote needed" and say from whom.
