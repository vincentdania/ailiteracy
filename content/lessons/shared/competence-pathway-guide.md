**Read time: 20 minutes · Nothing to install**

This pathway is not a set of videos you watch and forget. It is eight weeks of practical work, with feedback, that ends with you doing the job on your own in front of an independent assessor.

This lesson explains how that works. Read it before your baseline task, and come back to it whenever you're unsure what "good enough" means.

## What you'll have at the end

By the end of this lesson you'll know:

- the seven stages every pathway follows
- what you hand in each week, and what happens to it
- how your final assessment is scored, and what you must not fail
- what your certificate says, and what it doesn't

You'll also start your **competence record**, a single document where you track your evidence, feedback and revisions for the whole pathway.

## Competence, not completion

Most courses certify that you finished. This pathway certifies that you can **do the work**: produce a deliverable a real business or organisation could use, explain the choices you made, and handle a problem you haven't seen before.

> **New word: competence.** Being able to do a job to an agreed standard, reliably, without someone doing it for you. You prove it with evidence, not attendance.

That's why there are no marks for watching or attending. Every week you produce **evidence**: a map, a plan, a workflow, a dataset, a dashboard. Your mentor gives written feedback, you revise, and your evidence builds into a portfolio.

## The seven stages

Every pathway follows the same seven stages:

| Stage | What happens | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Define | The occupational outcomes, quality thresholds and evidence are set out for you | Before you start (this lesson and the standard in your baseline lesson) |
| 2. Diagnose | A short practical baseline task shows your strengths and gaps | Start here |
| 3. Develop | Principles, tools, ethics and safety | Weeks 1 to 6 |
| 4. Practise | Weekly evidence, written feedback, revision and repetition | Weeks 1 to 7 |
| 5. Specialise | An applied challenge for a partner business or realistic scenario | Week 7 |
| 6. Demonstrate | An unfamiliar task, judged by an independent assessor | Week 8 |
| 7. Deploy | Supervised real work with documented outcomes | After the pathway |

## Your week

Plan for **15 to 20 hours a week**. Each week follows the same five-day rhythm:

1. **Concepts and demonstration.** Read the week's lesson and watch the mentor's walk-through.
2. **Guided practice.** Work through the practice task in the lesson, with help available.
3. **Independent challenge.** Produce the week's evidence on your own.
4. **Critique and revision.** Your mentor gives written feedback. You revise and explain what you changed.
5. **Competence check.** A short check that you can explain and defend the work.

> **Stay safe:** Work only with synthetic data, or real data you have written permission to use with personal details removed. Never paste anyone's phone number, account number, health information or private messages into an AI tool that your organisation hasn't approved.

## What you hand in each week

Each week has two parts in its evidence brief:

- **Evidence.** The deliverable named in the curriculum, such as a customer journey map or a cleaning log, plus a short note on how you used AI and how you checked it.
- **Revision.** After feedback, your improved version and a short note on what you changed and why.

Hand in on time. Feedback only helps if there's time to act on it, and the revision is where most of the learning happens.

## How your work is assessed

Your mentor and the independent assessor use the same rubric. Each pathway may adjust the weights, but the shared rubric looks like this:

| Dimension | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Technical accuracy and completeness | 30% |
| Problem diagnosis and professional reasoning | 20% |
| Quality, usefulness and clarity of deliverable | 20% |
| Reproducibility, testing or measurement | 15% |
| Ethical practice, privacy and professional conduct | 10% |
| Oral defence and reflective improvement | 5% |

To be judged competent on the final assessment you need **80% overall**, and a pass on every **mandatory requirement**:

- **Critical safety.** Nothing you build or recommend could cause serious harm.
- **Ethics.** No fabricated claims, results, testimonials or quotes.
- **Data integrity.** Numbers trace back to their source, and changes to data are logged.
- **Authorization.** You only use data, accounts, money and messaging you were given permission to use.

A high score cannot make up for a mandatory fail. Borderline results are moderated by a second assessor, and every decision is written down.

> **If it didn't work:** Falling short isn't the end. You receive targeted feedback, practise the specific gaps, and can be reassessed.

## Using AI in this pathway

You'll use an AI assistant every week. It can draft, summarise, brainstorm and check, but **you** are responsible for every word and number you hand in. Three habits apply to every piece of evidence:

1. **Verify.** Check every fact, figure and claim against a source you can name.
2. **Disclose.** Keep a short AI-use log: what you asked, what you used, what you changed and how you checked it.
3. **Protect.** Remove personal information before anything goes near an AI tool.

Copy this AI-use log into your competence record and add a row every time AI contributes to your evidence:

```
AI-USE LOG
Date | Task | Tool | What I asked (short) | What I kept | What I changed or rejected | How I checked it
```

## What your certificate says

If you're judged competent, your certificate says you **demonstrated foundational competence** in your pathway on an **independent assessment**.

It does not say you're licensed, accredited or a senior professional, because eight weeks can't make you one, and Hyrax doesn't hold those licences or accreditations. Describe your result honestly: it's a strong, evidence-backed foundation that you build on with supervised real work.
