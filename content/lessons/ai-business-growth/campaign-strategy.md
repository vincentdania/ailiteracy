**Week 4 · About 15 to 20 hours · Competencies 2 and 4: Channels and campaigns**

A campaign is a short, focused effort to change one customer behaviour. This week you'll plan one end to end: what you want to happen, where you'll reach people, what you'll measure, what it costs and what the creative must do.

## What you'll be able to do

- Write SMART campaign objectives that serve a business goal
- Choose channels with reasons from your research
- Pick KPIs that measure the objective, not vanity
- Build a realistic budget with stop rules
- Write a creative brief someone else could work from

## Key ideas

**SMART objectives.** Specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound: "Get 12 quote requests from office buyers in Wuse 2 and Maitama between 1 and 28 February."

**Owned, earned and paid channels.** Owned: the business's own WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Business Profile, email list. Earned: referrals, reviews, word of mouth. Paid: ads. Small businesses usually get the best first results by fixing owned channels before paying for ads.

**The funnel and KPIs.** Choose one KPI per funnel stage, and one primary KPI tied to the objective:

| Stage | Example KPI |
| --- | --- |
| Reach | People who saw the post or ad |
| Engagement | Clicks to WhatsApp |
| Conversion (primary) | Quote requests from office buyers |
| Revenue | Confirmed orders and their value |

**Budget and stop rules.** Spread spend over tests, keep a reserve, and agree in advance when to stop: "Pause any ad set with no quote requests after ₦15,000."

## Worked example

```
CAMPAIGN PLAN: Zuma Bakes office trays (February)

Business goal: Grow office orders from about 2 a month to 6 a month by April.
Objective: 12 quote requests from office buyers in Wuse 2 and Maitama, 1–28 Feb.

Channels (and why)
1. Google Business Profile: competitors appear in Maps searches; we don't (scan).
2. LinkedIn posts from the owner: office admins and HR staff are there (interviews).
3. A one-page tray menu PDF sent on WhatsApp: removes the "DM for price" friction.
4. Paid: ₦30,000 test on Instagram ads, targeted to Wuse 2 and Maitama workplaces.

KPIs
Primary: quote requests tagged "office" in the WhatsApp Business label.
Secondary: Maps profile calls and direction requests; ad clicks to WhatsApp.

Budget (₦60,000, approved by owner on __ /__)
Canva Pro (1 month)       ₦  5,500
Printed menus for 2 offices ₦ 6,000
Instagram ads test        ₦ 30,000
Reserve                   ₦ 18,500
Stop rule: pause ads if no quote requests after ₦15,000 spend.

Risks: slow WhatsApp replies lose leads → owner commits to reply within 1 hour, 9am–5pm.
```


> **Stay safe:** Never ask the business for its ad account password. If you'll help set up ads next week, the owner should add you as a user with limited permissions, and stay the account owner.
