**Time: about 2 hours · You'll need a document and a spreadsheet**

Before you learn anything new, you'll show what you can already do. The baseline task takes about 90 minutes and it isn't scored. It tells you and your mentor where to focus over the next eight weeks.

## What you'll have at the end

- A **baseline growth diagnosis** for a small business case
- A **self-rating** against the five competencies
- Your **strengths and gaps** written into your competence record

## The competency standard

This is what you'll be able to do by week 8, and what the independent assessor will look for.

| Competency | At the foundation standard, you can... |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Customers and goals | Describe customers and business goals in measurable terms: who buys, why, and what number should change by when |
| 2. Research and channels | Run proportionate market research and choose channels with a reason, not a habit |
| 3. Content | Create marketing content that is accurate, appropriate for the audience, and accessible |
| 4. Campaigns | Set up and monitor an approved campaign safely, with the business controlling budget and accounts |
| 5. Measurement | Interpret conversion and acquisition metrics without overstating what caused the result |

Your partner project: help a small business improve **one clearly defined customer-acquisition or engagement process**. The business approves all spend and all communications.

## What this pathway is not

- It doesn't promise more sales. Good marketing improves the odds; it doesn't guarantee an outcome.
- It doesn't give you control of anyone's money or accounts. Budgets and ad accounts stay with the business.
- It doesn't make you a licensed or accredited marketer. It certifies foundational competence on an independent assessment.

## The baseline case

Read this case. It's fictional.

> **Zuma Bakes** is a two-year-old bakery in Wuse 2, Abuja, run by its owner and three staff. It sells celebration cakes, small chops and pastries. Most orders come through WhatsApp from people who already know the owner. Instagram has 1,900 followers; posts go out "when there's time". The owner says: "Offices around here order trays for meetings and birthdays, but they never order from us. I want more of that business. A friend told me to run ads, but I tried ₦15,000 of boosted posts last year and nothing happened."
>
> Known facts: average celebration cake ₦28,000; office small chops tray ₦45,000; about 6 office orders in the last 3 months, all from two companies. Unknown: monthly sales, profit margins, where boosted-post clicks went.

## The baseline task

Spend about 90 minutes. Use an AI assistant if you like, but log how you used it.

Write a two-page diagnosis with these five headings:

1. **The goal.** Rewrite the owner's wish as one measurable goal: a number, a starting point and a date. Say which numbers you'd need to confirm.
2. **The customer.** Who is the office buyer? Who decides, who pays, what worries them? List what you know, and what you'd need to find out.
3. **Why the boosted posts didn't work.** Give at least two possible reasons. Say what evidence would tell you which is true.
4. **Three ideas.** Three things the bakery could try in the next month, each with the cost, the risk and how you'd measure it.
5. **What you'd ask the owner.** Five questions before you'd spend any money.

> **Stay safe:** Don't invent numbers for the unknowns. Write "Unknown" and say how you'd find out. Inventing figures is a data integrity failure, even in a practice task.

## Rate yourself

After the task, rate yourself from 1 (I couldn't do this) to 4 (I could do this for a real client) on each competency:

```
SELF-RATING
1. Customers and goals:     1  2  3  4
2. Research and channels:   1  2  3  4
3. Content:                 1  2  3  4
4. Campaigns:               1  2  3  4
5. Measurement:             1  2  3  4

My strongest area:
The area I most want to improve:
```

Your mentor will rate your diagnosis against the same five competencies and you'll compare. Where the two ratings differ is usually where the most useful feedback is.
