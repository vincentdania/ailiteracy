**Week 7 · About 15 to 20 hours · All five competencies**

This week you specialise: you help a real partner business improve one clearly defined customer-acquisition or engagement process. Everything you've practised comes together, under the business's control and your mentor's supervision.

## What you'll be able to do

- Agree a tightly scoped brief with a partner business
- Produce campaign assets and get them approved before use
- Implement, monitor and adjust the campaign within the approved limits
- Document the work so it can be reviewed, handed over or repeated

## Key ideas

**One process, clearly defined.** "Help the business grow" is too big. "Increase quote requests from office buyers through the Google Business Profile and a tray menu" is a project you can finish and measure.

**Approval gates.** Before anything that spends money or speaks for the business, the owner approves. Agree how approval works: who, how quickly, and in what form (a WhatsApp reply is fine if you keep a record).

**Implementation log.** A running record of what you did, when, with whose approval, and what happened. It protects you and the client.

**Handover.** At the end, the business should be able to keep going without you: which accounts you touched, what's scheduled, what to watch, and how to remove your access.

## Implementation log

```
IMPLEMENTATION LOG
Date | Action | Approved by (and how) | Result | Problem / fix
```

## Use AI well this week

AI can save you time on the routine parts: variations of approved copy, summaries of the week for the owner, a first draft of handover notes. Every output still goes through the same checks: facts, tone, claims and approval.

```
Draft a short weekly update for a bakery owner from these notes (pasted, no
personal data). Plain language, under 150 words: what we did, what happened,
what's next, and what I need them to approve.
```

> **Stay safe:** Remove your own access at handover unless the owner asks you to keep it, in writing. Never keep copies of customer lists or chats after the project.

## Your evidence this week

Hand in, as described in the week 7 evidence brief:

1. The signed **partner brief**
2. **Approved campaign assets**, each with the date and form of approval
3. The **implementation log**
4. **Handover notes** for the business
5. A **testimonial or feedback** from the owner, only with their permission (optional)

What the assessor looks for: a clear scope, approval before every external action, a complete log, and a business that can continue without you.
