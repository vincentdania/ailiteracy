**Week 8 · Independent competence assessment**

This week you show, on your own, that you can do the work. An independent assessor gives you a business case you haven't seen, and judges your response against the same rubric your mentor has used all pathway.

## How the assessment works

1. **The unseen brief.** You receive an unfamiliar business case. It's not one you've worked on.
2. **Your response.** In the time given (usually one working day), you produce:
   - an **evidence-backed growth plan**: goal, segments, channels, objectives, KPIs and budget
   - **sample creatives**: at least two pieces of copy and one visual
   - a **measurement approach**: how you'd track and judge the results honestly
   - a **risk register**: what could go wrong, how likely, how serious, and what you'd do
3. **The oral defence.** About 30 minutes. You present briefly, then the assessor challenges your assumptions and asks for revisions. You may be asked to change part of your plan on the spot.

You may use an AI assistant and your own notes. Log your AI use as usual.

## How you're judged

| Dimension | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Technical accuracy and completeness | 30% |
| Problem diagnosis and professional reasoning | 20% |
| Quality, usefulness and clarity of deliverable | 20% |
| Reproducibility, testing or measurement | 15% |
| Ethical practice, privacy and professional conduct | 10% |
| Oral defence and reflective improvement | 5% |

To be judged competent: **80% overall** and a pass on every mandatory requirement: no fabricated claims, figures or testimonials; no unsafe or unauthorised spend or messaging; all figures traced to the brief or marked as assumptions; privacy protected.

> **If it didn't work:** If you're judged "not yet competent", you get written feedback on the specific gaps, a short remediation plan and a reassessment date.

## Practice brief

Rehearse with this case under timed conditions. It's fictional.

> **Kemi's Fabrics** sells Ankara and Aso-oke fabric from a stall in Garki Market, Abuja, and has 3,400 followers on Instagram. Sales are steady from regular market customers, but the owner wants to sell to tailors and fashion designers outside Abuja who could order in bulk. She can spend up to ₦100,000 over two months and ships through a dispatch company. Known: average bulk order would be about ₦180,000; 5 out-of-town tailors have ordered once in the past year, all via Instagram DMs. Unknown: repeat rate, profit margin, shipping complaints.

Produce the four deliverables, then ask a fellow learner or your mentor to play the assessor using the questions below.

## Prepare for the oral defence

Expect questions like these. Practise answering each in under two minutes:

- What evidence is your main channel choice based on? What would change your mind?
- Which number in your plan are you least sure of? How would you find out?
- If results come in at half your target after two weeks, what do you do?
- What could go wrong for the customers, not just for the business?
- Where did you use AI, and how did you check it?
- If you had half the budget, what would you cut, and why?

## Assemble your graduate portfolio

Your portfolio is the lasting evidence of what you can do. Collect the final, revised version of each item:

| Item | From |
| --- | --- |
| Market research memo | Week 2 |
| Customer profiles | Weeks 1 and 2 |
| Campaign plan | Week 4 |
| Creative pack | Weeks 3 and 7 |
| Measurement dashboard | Week 6 |
| Reflection on results | Week 7 and this week |

Before you share it anywhere:

- Replace partner business names with a code name unless they've agreed in writing to be named
- Remove every customer name, phone number, email and account ID
- Get the business's permission before publishing any work sample

> **Stay safe:** Your portfolio must never imply a licence or accreditation you don't hold, or a guaranteed result. Describe your certificate as "demonstrated foundational competence, independently assessed".

## After the pathway

Competence grows with supervised real work. Good next steps include performance marketing, content strategy, growth analytics, creative production and marketing automation. Keep your competence record going: add each new piece of real work and what you learned from it.
