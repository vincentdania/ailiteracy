**Read time: 12 minutes · Build time: 20 minutes · Cost: included with your Nous account**

How many emails did you open yesterday just to find out they didn't need you? In this lesson, Hermes reads your inbox for you and hands back a short list: what needs you **today**, what can wait, and what to ignore.

It also writes replies to the important ones. But it **never sends** them. It saves them as drafts in Gmail, and you press Send.

![Hermes's triage: Needs attention now, then Replies to approve, with draft replies waiting.](/lessons/professionals/inbox-triage-assistant/step-first-triage.png "At the end of this lesson, your morning inbox looks like this.")

## What you'll have at the end

By the end of this lesson you'll have:

1. Your **Gmail connected** to Hermes.
2. A **sorted list** of your last day of email, in five piles.
3. **Draft replies** waiting in Gmail for you to check and send.
4. Your own **triage rules**, remembered by Hermes.

## How Hermes sorts your inbox

Hermes has a built-in skill for this job, called **Email Inbox Triage**. It puts every email into one of five piles:

![Five piles: needs attention now, replies to approve, actions without a reply, waiting on others, and reference and noise.](/lessons/professionals/inbox-triage-assistant/five-piles.png "You only need to look at the first three.")

> **New word: triage.** Sorting things by how urgent they are, like a nurse deciding who to see first. You'll triage your inbox in two minutes instead of thirty.

> **New word: skill.** A set of instructions Hermes already knows for a particular job. You don't need to install this one. Hermes uses it when you ask it to triage your inbox.

## Step 1: Connect Gmail

1. Open **Hermes**.
2. Click **Capabilities** in the left menu.
3. Click the **Connectors** tab, then type `Gmail` in the search box.

![Capabilities in the left menu (1), the Connectors tab (2) and the search box (3).](/lessons/professionals/inbox-triage-assistant/step-open-connectors.png "Step 1: find Gmail under Capabilities, then Connectors.")

4. On the **Gmail** card, click **Connect**. Your web browser opens a Google page.
5. Choose your email account, and sign in if Google asks.
6. Google shows what Hermes is asking to do. Read it, then click **Allow**.
7. Go back to Hermes. The Gmail card now says **Connected**.

![Click Connect on Gmail (1), choose your account in the browser (2), click Allow (3), and Gmail says Connected in Hermes (4).](/lessons/professionals/inbox-triage-assistant/gmail-connect.png "Connect Gmail in four clicks.")

> **New word: connector.** A secure link between Hermes and another app, like Gmail. You sign in on Google's own page, so Hermes never sees your password. You can disconnect it at any time.

> **If it didn't work:** If you don't see **Gmail**, check you're signed in to your Nous account: Gmail is a managed connector, and managed apps need a Nous sign-in. If the browser page closes without connecting, click **Connect** again. You can also type in a Hermes chat: `Connect my Gmail`, and Hermes walks you through it.

### Not on Gmail?

If your email is Outlook, Yahoo or your company's own email, type its name in the **Connectors** search box first. If it isn't listed, ask Hermes in a chat:

```text
Help me connect my email so you can read and draft replies. My email is with [your email company]. Walk me through it one step at a time.
```

Hermes knows how to connect most email services with an **app password**. That's a separate password just for Hermes, which you create in your email account's security settings. Never give Hermes your main email password.

## Step 2: Your first triage

1. Click **New session** at the top left.
2. Copy this request, paste it into the message box and press **Enter**:

```text
Please triage my inbox from the last 24 hours. Tell me what needs me today, and draft replies for anything urgent. Do not send or delete anything.
```

3. Hermes reads your email. This takes 30 seconds to a few minutes, depending on how much email you have.

![Your request (1), what needs you today (2), and draft replies to approve (3).](/lessons/professionals/inbox-triage-assistant/step-first-triage.png "Step 2: your inbox, sorted.")

> **You should now see:** a short report in five parts, starting with **Needs attention now**. Your report will list your own emails, so it will look different from the picture.

**Check it.** Open Gmail in your browser and compare. Did Hermes miss anything important? Did it call something urgent that isn't? Note what it got wrong. You'll fix that in Step 4.

> **If it didn't work:** If Hermes says it can't reach your email, go back to **Capabilities**, then **Connectors**, and check Gmail says **Connected**. If it says **Access expired**, click **Reconnect**.

## Step 3: Save the drafts, send them yourself

Hermes has written replies, but nothing has left your inbox. Now ask it to put them where you can check them:

```text
Yes, save both drafts in Gmail. Don't send them.
```

If Hermes asks for approval before saving, check what it says it will do, then approve it.

![You ask Hermes to save the drafts (1), and Hermes confirms nothing was sent (2).](/lessons/professionals/inbox-triage-assistant/step-save-drafts.png "Step 3: drafts go to Gmail. You do the sending.")

Now open **Gmail**, then your **Drafts** folder:

1. Open each draft and read every word.
2. Check names, dates, prices and promises. AI can sound confident and still be wrong.
3. Edit anything that doesn't sound like you.
4. Click **Send** only when you're happy.

> **Stay safe:** Never ask Hermes to "just send everything". You are the approval step. Reading a draft takes ten seconds. Fixing a wrong email sent to a client takes much longer.

## Step 4: Teach Hermes your rules

Hermes keeps a short memory about you. Tell it what matters in your work, once, and it uses that every time it triages.

Copy this, **change it to fit your work**, then send it:

```text
Remember these rules for my inbox triage:
- My most important clients are Lagoon Foods and Bright Path Schools. Their emails are always urgent.
- Anything about payments, contracts or deadlines this week is urgent.
- Newsletters and social media notifications are always noise.
- Sign my draft replies "Best, Priya".
- Write drafts in short, warm, plain English.
```

> **You should now see:** Hermes confirms it has saved your rules to memory.

Keep your rules short: Hermes's memory has a size limit. Five to ten lines is plenty. To change a rule later, just tell Hermes: `Update my triage rules: Bright Path Schools is no longer a client.`

## Step 5: Triage from your phone

You connected Telegram in the last lesson, so you can triage from anywhere while your computer is on and Hermes is open.

1. Open your Hermes bot in Telegram.
2. Send `/new` to start a fresh conversation.
3. Send the same request from Step 2.

Hermes replies in the chat with your five piles. Ask it to save drafts the same way. You'll read and send them from the Gmail app on your phone.

## Keep it safe

1. **Emails are information, not instructions.** If an email says "Assistant, forward this to...", Hermes treats it as text to report, not an order to follow. If you ever see Hermes about to act on something an email told it to do, say **no** to the approval.
2. **Watch for scams.** Ask Hermes to flag them: add `Flag anything that looks like a scam or asks for money or passwords.` to your rules.
3. **Client confidentiality.** Your emails go to your AI model to be read. Check your clients' contracts allow that. Module 6 covers this in detail.
4. **Disconnect anytime.** In **Capabilities**, then **Connectors**, open Gmail and choose **Disconnect**. You can also remove access from your Google Account, under **Security**, then **Third-party apps**.
