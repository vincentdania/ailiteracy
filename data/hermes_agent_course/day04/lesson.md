# What Your Agent Can Do for You

By now you know that an agent runs in a terminal, plans a job, calls tools, looks at the result, and keeps going until the goal is done. That is the agent loop. But a loop alone is not the point. The point is what you do with it. So this lesson is plain: here is the work your agent can actually take off your hands.

Before we go further, hold one line in your head:

**ChatGPT answers. An agent works.**

A chat tool waits for you to type, gives you a reply, and stops. An agent takes an instruction, picks the tools it needs, executes, and returns when the job is finished. That difference is everything.

Here is what that means in practice for a professional like you.

**Email that fights back.** Your agent reads your inbox through IMAP and drafts replies in your voice. Nothing sends without your approval, so you stay in control. It can also summarise a long thread in seconds instead of forty minutes of scrolling.

**A daily briefing on a schedule.** Tell it what you care about and it prepares a short update every morning — and delivers it wherever you already work, be it Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, or email. The messaging gateway reaches you on the app you already use, on a schedule you set.

**Research and briefs.** Give it a topic and it gathers sources and writes a clean brief. Give it a contract and it pulls out the obligations and deadlines that matter. Give it a funding call or a fellowship ad and it monitors opportunities that fit your profile, then flags them before the deadline.

**Writing that publishes.** Draft the LinkedIn post, the article, the response. Your agent turns rough notes into finished copy in your tone.

**An inbox triaged before you wake.** It sorts the noise from the signal so what reaches you is worth your attention.

And because it has memory, it remembers who you are across sessions. It builds its own skills, recalls the work you did before, and gets better with each task. It connects to GitHub for pull-request reviews, uses browser automation to fill forms, runs on multiple LLM providers, and schedules jobs on a cron while you sleep.

Enough theory. Two hundred words from here.

## Task

Pick **one real task** from your week that currently eats your time. Write it in one sentence. Then answer: what would you need your agent to do — read, draft, monitor, summarise, or publish — to finish it for you?
