# What an Agent Actually Is

**Read time: 10 minutes · Task: 10 minutes**

A chatbot answers. An **agent** works — on instructions, with memory, on a schedule. Today you learn the four parts, see one working, and turn your Playbook into a standing assistant.

## The honest mental model

A chatbot answers a question and stops. An agent has four things a chatbot does not:

1. **Standing instructions** — its role and rules, stated once, applied every time.
2. **Memory** — your context, your templates, your way of writing.
3. **Tools** — what it may use (a document, a spreadsheet, a calendar).
4. **A trigger** — when it works: every Monday, before the meeting, when you ask.

Nothing mystical about this. That is exactly what a good personal assistant is: briefed once, remembers the job, knows the tools, shows up on time.

## Harness, in plain words

Around every working agent there is **scaffolding** that holds the instructions, the memory and the routine together. Engineers call it a harness. Here is the honest part: **you have been building one all week.** Your Playbook is the beginnings of a harness — instructions, memory, rules and checks, written down, in your pocket.

## See one working

No recording, no video to watch — you build the demonstration yourself, in five minutes, on the phone in your hand. Take the persona block below into a fresh chat and run one real work request. Then run the **same** request in a chat with no persona at all. Compare the two answers.

The second is a chatbot. The first is an assistant that arrived already briefed. What you build today and tomorrow is the free-tools version: the same four parts, on your own phone.

## The conversion move

The move today: paste your Playbook as an instruction set, so every new chat starts already briefed.

**Method 1 — platform features, if available (optional):** ChatGPT has *custom instructions*; Gemini has *Saved Info*. Whether these are available and big enough on the free tier may vary — check your app. If you find the feature, paste the persona block below into it and save.

**Method 2 — paste-the-persona (manual fallback, subject to service availability — the primary method):** keep the persona block as the first item in your Playbook. Open each new work chat and paste it in before your request. One extra paste, and the AI arrives briefed.

**Copy-paste persona block:**

> You are my work assistant. My role: [YOUR ROLE, e.g. admin officer in a state ministry / small business owner / classroom teacher]. My rules: [YOUR PRIVACY RULES — what you never paste; always verify facts before I use them]. My formats: [YOUR OUTPUT FORMATS — memos in my ministry format, customer replies in a warm tone, reports with headings and action points]. When I give you a request, follow my formats and rules without me repeating them. Ask me one clarifying question if my request is genuinely unclear.

The memory file is simply your Playbook note itself — you can paste relevant sections into a chat when the task needs them.

## Worked example: briefing a shop assistant

*Illustrative, not a connected autonomous agent.*

Input: “I sell stationery. Confirmed stock today: blue pens only. Collection from the shop; no delivery service.” Prompt: “Act as my drafting assistant. Never invent stock or delivery. Reply to: Do you have red pens and can you deliver?”

Sample output: “We currently have blue pens, not red ones. Collection is available from the shop; we do not offer delivery. Would blue pens suit you?”

Check the stock before sending. The chat has not read your inventory, sent a message or booked delivery. You supplied its context and remain responsible for the action.

## 🎯 Task

1. Install your Playbook as custom instructions **or** save the persona block and paste it (the free fallback — the primary method on any phone).
2. Run one real work request with the persona in place.
3. Compare the answer with the same kind of request from Day 1, before the assistant was briefed. Notice what you no longer have to repeat.

## 📤 Output

One briefed run: the same AI, now carrying your role, rules and formats from the first line. That difference is the foundation for tomorrow, when you give it a standing job.
