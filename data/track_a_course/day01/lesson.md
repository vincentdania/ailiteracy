# From Meeting Notes to a Finished Report in 10 Minutes

**Read time: 10–12 minutes · Task: 10 minutes**

This course is not about AI theory. It is about your work. By the end of today you will have turned rough notes into a finished, formatted report — the kind of thing that normally takes you an hour of careful typing.

## What this course is and is not

It **is**: one short lesson and one real task every day, using the actual work you already have on your desk or in your phone.

It **is not**: coding, installing software, or a lecture about how AI works. You will never be asked to write a line of code in this course. Everything runs on free tools — ChatGPT (free tier) or Gemini (free tier) — on the phone or laptop you already own.

There are no income or job-placement promises here either. What we promise is simpler and honest: every day, you finish with something you actually made.

## Opening ChatGPT or Gemini, in under a minute

1. Open your browser and go to **chatgpt.com** or **gemini.google.com**.
2. Sign up with your email or Google account — the free option. That is all you need. No card, no download required (though the apps work too).
3. A typing box appears. That box is your workplace for the next ten days.

## The only three ideas you need today

1. **Give the AI your real material.** Paste your actual notes, not a vague description of them. AI is a worker, not a mind-reader — a worker with empty hands does nothing.
2. **Say exactly who the output is for.** "For my Head of Service," "for parents," "for a customer" changes the words the AI chooses.
3. **Tell it the format you want.** Headings? Bullet points? Length? If you don't say, it guesses — and its guesses are generic.

Everything else in this course is these three ideas, repeated with more skill.

## The worked demo

Below are messy notes from a real meeting, followed by the exact prompt used — word for word — and what came back.

**The messy notes:**

> staff meeting 14 Aug — 9 people present. new resumption time 8am from Sept, Mrs Eze to circulate memo. borehole repair quoted 380k, wait for 2nd quote. Friday clean-up continues. Ada to follow up on training forms, 6 staff yet to submit. AOB: NEPA bill unpaid, threat of disconnection, Mr Bala handling.

**The exact prompt used:**

```
You are an experienced administrative officer. Below are my rough notes
from a staff meeting. Turn them into a clean report for my Head of
Department.

Use this format:
- A one-line heading with the meeting date
- "Attendees:" line
- "Matters arising" as short numbered points
- "Action points" as a list in this form: [task] — [owner name]

Keep it under 200 words. Do not invent anything that is not in the notes.

NOTES:
[paste your notes here]
```

**What came back** was a properly headed report with attendees listed, four numbered matters arising, and an action-point list naming Mrs Eze, Ada and Mr Bala against their tasks. Ten minutes of typing, done in one paste.

## The same template, six different jobs

One template serves every role. The notes change; the structure does not.

- **Civil servant:** paste your meeting minutes → official report for the permanent secretary.
- **Teacher:** paste staff-briefing notes → a clean briefing summary for colleagues who were absent.
- **SME owner:** paste notes from a customer call → a summary of what was agreed, for your records and theirs.
- **NGO officer:** paste field-visit notes → an activity report for your programme officer.
- **Banker:** paste branch meeting notes → a formatted update for your team lead.
- **Jobseeker:** paste notes from a career fair → a summary of who you met, what they said, and your follow-ups.

## 🎯 Task

Take notes from a real meeting, class, or customer conversation you attended — on your phone, in your notes app, or in a notebook. Then:

1. Open ChatGPT or Gemini (free).
2. Paste the template above with your own notes in the slot.
3. Read the report it produces. Then try the follow-up prompts: "Make it shorter," "Make it more formal," "Add action points."
4. Compare it with what you would have written by hand. Notice where it is right, and where it left something out — you will fix that instinct tomorrow.

## 📤 Output

One finished report from your own notes. Keep it — it is the first page of your Personal AI Playbook (Day 7). Save the template to your phone notes; you will use it for years.
