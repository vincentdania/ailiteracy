# Your Next Move

**Read time: 8 minutes · Task: 12 minutes**

Everything you have learned this week — give real material, say who it's for, name the format, verify the output — now points at one forward step in *your* actual situation. This lesson branches into three tracks. Read your track only. The task is real work you owe someone, done faster.

## Track A — CV, Cover Letter and Interview Preparation (jobseekers and professionals)

**1. Turn duties into achievements.** Your CV says "responsible for filing records." It should say what changed because you did it. Paste your CV and use this prompt:

> You are an experienced Nigerian HR officer. Here is my CV: [PASTE]. Rewrite each work experience bullet so it describes an achievement, not a duty. Keep it truthful — do not invent numbers I have not given you. Ask me up to 3 questions if you need facts to complete any bullet.

**2. Tailor one CV to one real advert.** Paste the job advert and ask:

> Here is the job advert: [PASTE]. Compare it with my CV above. List the top 5 requirements they want, then rewrite my CV profile (the short paragraph at the top) and my bullet points to match — using only true things from my CV.

**3. A cover letter that reads human.** Short, specific, one page. Ask for a cover letter that names the exact role, one real achievement, and why this employer. Then edit it yourself — your voice must survive.

**4. Interview rehearsal.** Paste the job description and prompt:

> You are the hiring manager for this role. Ask me 5 likely interview questions one at a time. After each of my answers, tell me one thing that was strong and one thing to improve. Be direct.

**5. The job-scam check — do this before you apply.** AI can help you check, but the rules are simple and fixed:

- No genuine employer asks you to **pay a recruitment fee**, pay for "training materials", or buy airtime to "confirm interest". Any fee at all = walk away.
- Verify the company exists: search the name plus "CAC", check their real website and a real person you can call. Be suspicious of a Gmail address doing the hiring for a big company.
- A salary far above the market rate for the role, with an easy interview, is the oldest bait there is.

## Track B — Proposals and Concept Notes (NGO, programme and development staff)

**1. The concept-note skeleton.** Most donors and your own organisation want the same spine: problem · objectives · activities · budget lines · monitoring and evaluation. Extract your organisation's format from an old document first (the Day 3 template-extraction move), then draft new sections against it.

**2. Draft against the donor's language.** Paste the call for proposals or the donor's priority text and prompt:

> Here is the donor's call: [PASTE]. Here is my draft section: [PASTE]. Rewrite my section using the donor's own priority language and phrases, without inventing activities we have not planned or figures we do not have.

**3. Logframe-assisted drafting.** Ask AI to draft a simple results table — activities, outputs, indicators, means of verification — then check every indicator yourself. AI proposes, you decide.

**4. Field data into reportable results.** Paste anonymised field notes and ask: "Summarise this into 5 reportable results with numbers, using only the figures in my notes."

## Track C — Lesson Plans and Teaching Materials (teachers and lecturers)

**1. Lesson plan in your school's format.** Paste one old approved lesson plan and the topic for next week:

> Here is my school's lesson plan format: [PASTE]. Prepare next week's lesson for [CLASS] on [TOPIC] in exactly this format, with differentiation for weak learners in the development stage.

**2. Differentiated notes.** For mixed-ability classes, ask for the same lesson at three levels: core explanation, simpler version with a local example, and an extension task for fast finishers.

**3. Marking feedback that is specific and kind.** Paste anonymised student answers and prompt:

> Here are students' answers on [TOPIC] (anonymised). For each, write feedback with one specific thing done well and one specific improvement. Kind but honest tone. Then give me 3 whole-class teaching points from the common errors.

**4. Revision questions and marking guides.** Paste your syllabus or scheme of work and ask for revision questions with answers — then verify each answer before you teach it. The Day 2 four-question check is not optional in a classroom.

## Worked examples for the three branches

*These are illustrative teaching examples, not learner results.*

- **Jobseeker:** Input: “Filed weekly stock records and followed up missing entries; no measured improvement recorded.” Prompt: “Write one truthful CV bullet. Do not invent a percentage.” Sample: “Maintained weekly stock records and followed up missing entries.” Check that you actually performed both actions; an honest duty is better than a fabricated achievement.
- **Programme officer:** Input: “Two sessions held; 18 attendees at the first and 16 at the second; repeat attendance unknown.” Prompt: “Draft one results sentence and identify the counting limitation.” Sample: “Two sessions recorded 34 attendances; the number of unique participants has not been established.” Check attendance registers before claiming 34 people reached.
- **Teacher:** Input: “Primary 4; fractions; 35 minutes; bottle tops available.” Prompt: “Create an introduction, guided activity and three assessment questions using bottle tops. Do not assume internet access.” Sample activity: arrange eight tops and identify one-half and one-quarter. Check the answers yourself and match the activity to your approved scheme of work.

## 🎯 Task

Follow your track:

- **Track A:** tailor your CV to one real advert and run one mock interview (answers written down, not spoken into the air).
- **Track B:** draft one real section of a proposal or report you actually owe this month.
- **Track C:** produce next week's lesson plan — or marking feedback for one real set of student work, anonymised.

One finished work product from your own job — the same thing you would have spent an evening on, done in minutes, and checked by you.
