# Your Personal AI Playbook

**Read time: 10 minutes · Task: 10 minutes**

Seven days of work, sitting in your chat history, ready to disappear the moment you open a new conversation. Today you pull it all into one document that stays with you: your **Personal AI Playbook**.

## The Playbook format

One note or document on your phone (your notes app is fine) with these sections:

- **Reports** — your meeting-notes-to-report template from Day 1, plus your decisions-summary prompt from Day 5.
- **Emails & messages** — your answered-email skeleton and customer-reply set from Day 4.
- **Numbers** — your spreadsheet-builder prompt from Day 5.
- **My-role documents** — the format-following and template-extraction prompts from Day 3, plus your Day 6 work product.
- **My privacy rules** — the "safe to paste / never paste" card from Day 2, in your own words.
- **My verification checklist** — the four questions: Does it name a source? Does the source exist? Do the numbers add up? Would I say this to my boss with my name on it?

Nothing fancy. A plain note with those six headings and your actual prompts under each.

## The 10-minute Playbook review ritual

Once a week — same day, same time, ten minutes:

1. Look at what AI produced for you this week.
2. **Keep** what worked — it is already in the Playbook.
3. **Fix one weak prompt.** If an output missed the mark, add one sentence of instruction to that prompt so it lands next time.

That third step is the habit that makes this course permanent. Tools change monthly; your Playbook is the thing that compounds.

## Keeping current without chasing hype

You will hear about a new AI tool every few weeks. Ignore most of it. The tools change; the **moves** do not: give real material, specify role and format, verify the output. Every new tool you ever meet is operated with those same three moves. Test new tools against your Playbook, not against hype.

## Where to go next

One short section, honestly: the only thing between you and mastery now is **practice** — the ritual above, run weekly, on your real work. The next three days (Days 8–10, Agents Week — included in this course, no extra payment) show you how to turn the Playbook into an assistant with instructions, memory and a routine that produces work on a schedule.

## 🎯 Task

1. Assemble your Playbook from the week's work — all six sections, your real prompts.
2. Test **two** of its prompts once more, fresh, end to end. If a result disappoints, fix the prompt in the Playbook before submitting.
3. Submit the Playbook.
