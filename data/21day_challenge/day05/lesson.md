# Day 5 — Which Tool for Which Job

**Read time: 5 minutes · Task: 15 minutes**

You've now touched all five tools and built a library of prompts. Today you settle the question that nags everyone: which tool should I actually use? The answer may surprise you: it matters less than you think.

## The quick comparison

- **ChatGPT** — general all-rounder. Everyday drafting, brainstorming, quick answers. When in doubt, start here.
- **Claude** — long documents and careful writing. Upload a 40-page report and ask questions; get thoughtful, well-structured drafts. Its Projects and Artifacts get the full treatment on Day 12.
- **Gemini** — Google's ecosystem. Best if you live in Gmail, Drive, Docs and Sheets.
- **Copilot** — inside Office. Best when the work lives in Word, Excel or PowerPoint and you want help without leaving the app.
- **Perplexity** — cited research. Best when you need sources and current information, not just an answer.

## There is no best tool

Tools are not loyalties. They are free to switch between, and the switching cost is zero. Some weeks ChatGPT does everything; other weeks Claude reads your 40-page contract, Gemini answers from your Drive folder, and Perplexity nails the research. Use all of them. The only wrong move is marrying one and defending it like a football club.

## A starting rule of thumb

Long or messy documents → Claude. Research and facts → Perplexity. Office work → Copilot. Everything else → ChatGPT. That's a starting point, not a doctrine — and today you'll refine it with evidence instead of opinions.

## Why this matters

Tool anxiety is wasted energy. Nobody at work cares which assistant you used; they care whether the work is good. When you can reach for whichever tool fits the job without agonising, you've stopped thinking about tools entirely — which is exactly where you want to be at the end of Phase 1.

---

## 🎯 Task (15 minutes)

Take one real deliverable from your work — an email, a summary, a short report — and produce it in TWO different tools using the same prompt. Compare: which is clearer? Which sounds more like you? Which needed less editing?

*Example: "Same prompt, two tools, one verdict. ChatGPT vs Claude is the classic pairing, but any two work."*

## 📤 Output

Your **Tool Selection Cheat Sheet**: the five tools, the jobs you'll use each for, and your default "start here" tool. One page, bullet points. Phase 1 is done — you understand AI, you can prompt it, and you know your tools. Tomorrow Phase 2 begins: no more practice. From Day 6 you'll use AI to do real work — starting with writing and drafting.
