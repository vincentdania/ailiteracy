# Day 2 — Your AI Toolkit

**Read time: 5 minutes · Task: 15 minutes**

Yesterday you learned what AI can and can't do. Today you get the tools in your hands — five of them, all free, all set up in about fifteen minutes.

## Meet your toolkit

You don't need a dozen apps. You need five, and every one has a genuinely usable free tier. No API, no developer skills, no credit card. Here's the landscape, one line per tool:

- **ChatGPT** — the all-rounder. Best for everyday work: drafting, brainstorming, quick answers. Your default starting point.
- **Claude** — the careful writer. Best for long documents, nuanced writing, and files you upload.
- **Gemini** — Google's assistant. Best if your working life runs on Gmail, Drive and Docs.
- **Copilot** — the Office helper. Lives inside Word, Excel and PowerPoint, so you get help without leaving the document. Available through many workplace Microsoft 365 plans; if yours doesn't include it, skip it for now — nothing in this course depends on it.
- **Perplexity** — the researcher. Best for questions where you want to see the sources, not just the answer.

## Free tier, real limits

Be honest with yourself about free tiers: ChatGPT and Claude cap how many messages you can send in a few hours, and Perplexity limits how many searches you get. When you hit a cap, wait — or switch tools, since switching costs nothing. The caps exist so the free ride stays free.

## Pick one primary tool

For the next few days, do your practice in ONE tool — ChatGPT is the sensible default — and keep the others on standby. Fluency beats tool-hopping. You'll run the head-to-head comparison on Day 5, and that's the day you pick your stack for real.

## Why this matters

You can't judge a tool from reviews; you judge it from use. Fifteen minutes today gives you accounts that cost nothing and muscle memory you'll rely on for the next nineteen days. And if you post one thing from today, make it this: your entire AI toolkit costs you nothing but attention.

---

## 🎯 Task (15 minutes)

1. Create free accounts at chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and perplexity.ai. (Sign in to Gemini with a Google account.)
2. If you use Word or Excel at work, check whether Copilot is available in your apps. If not, skip it.
3. Run the same test prompt in each tool: "Explain what you are and the one thing you're best at, in three lines."
4. Note one sentence on each: which felt easiest? Which gave the most useful answer?

*Example: "ChatGPT — easiest. Claude — most thoughtful. Gemini — fine. Perplexity — gave me sources I could check. Copilot — not on my licence yet."*

## 📤 Output

Your **Toolkit Note**: the five tools, your one-line impression of each, and your pick for primary tool. A phone note or a screenshot is fine. Keep it — you'll update it on Day 5 after the head-to-head, and it becomes part of your AI Work Playbook on Day 21.
