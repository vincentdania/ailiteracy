# Build Your Personal AI Operating System

You now have individual AI skills. This bonus lab connects them into one system you can reuse every week.

## Your three-part operating system

1. **Capture:** Keep one list of repetitive, language-heavy work.
2. **Prompt:** Save the prompt, context and quality checklist that produced a good result.
3. **Review:** Spend ten minutes each Friday improving one workflow.

## Bonus task

Choose one recurring task and document its trigger, inputs, prompt, verification checklist and final owner. Run it once, measure the time saved, then teach it to one colleague.
