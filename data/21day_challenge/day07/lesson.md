# Day 7 — Research and Summarising

**Read time: 5 minutes · Task: 15 minutes**

Reading is the slowest part of knowledge work. A 40-page report. A 20-page contract. A policy document nobody has time to finish. Today, AI reads them for you — and you learn the one habit that keeps that safe.

## Upload the document

In ChatGPT or Claude, attach the file — PDF or Word — right in the chat. Free tiers support file uploads. Then ask. You don't read the document; you interrogate it.

## What to ask a document

- "Summarise this in five bullet points."
- "What are the key decisions and deadlines?"
- "What are the action items, and who owns them?"
- "What risks or concerns does it raise?"
- "Explain section 3 in plain English."

The trick is to ask follow-ups like you would of a colleague who has read it: "Which deadline is most urgent?" "What did they actually say about the budget?"

## The verification habit

AI can misread, skip or invent — so the Day 1 rule still applies. Ask for sources: "Which page or section does that claim come from?" Then check the ones that matter. This one habit — always asking where it came from — is what separates professionals who use AI for research from people who get embarrassed by it later.

## The one-page brief

This is the goal state: any document you're handed becomes a one-page brief in minutes — summary, key points, action items, open questions. If you can do that, you become the person everyone sends the hard documents to.

## Why this matters

Half a senior professional's reading load is documents that could be a one-page brief. When you can digest a 40-page report in minutes — and back every claim with a section number — you're not just faster, you're a different category of worker. That's how you get noticed.

---

## 🎯 Task (15 minutes)

Take a real document of 10+ pages from your work — a report, a policy, a contract, even a long email thread. Upload it to ChatGPT or Claude, then:

1. Get a one-page summary.
2. Extract a five-item action list.
3. Ask for section references on your two most important points — and check them.

*Example: "No document handy? Use a public report or a long article you've been meaning to read. Real work is better."*

## 📤 Output

Your **1-Page Summary + Action List**: the summary, the five actions, and your verification notes. Tomorrow you'll stop feeding AI documents and start feeding it decisions — the day it becomes your thinking partner.
