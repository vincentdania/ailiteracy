# Day 4 — Prompting Practice: 10 Real Work Tasks

**Read time: 5 minutes · Task: 15 minutes**

Yesterday was theory. Today is reps. You're going to run ten real work tasks through AI in one sitting, until prompting stops being something you think about and becomes something you just do.

## The ten tasks

These ten types cover most of what any professional does with words. One worked example each — then you run them on your own work:

- **Email** — "Draft a polite but firm reply to a client chasing a payment that's late because of our supplier."
- **Summary** — "Summarise these meeting notes in four bullet points."
- **Rewrite** — "Make this paragraph more direct: [paste your text]."
- **Brainstorm** — "Give me ten subject lines for the launch email for our new course."
- **Plan** — "Break 'launch the new product page' into a two-week plan with owners."
- **Explain** — "Explain what a VPN does as if I'm a smart 15-year-old."
- **Translate** — "Translate this for a UK client, keeping the professional tone: [paste]."
- **Outline** — "Give me a six-section outline for a quarterly business review."
- **Bulletise** — "Turn this paragraph into five crisp bullet points."
- **Decide** — "Give me five pros and five cons of outsourcing payroll versus keeping it in-house."

## Reps build fluency

None of these is hard on its own. Doing all ten back to back with your own content is what builds fluency. By the fifth you'll stop reading prompts and start writing them; by the tenth, your early drafts will look clumsy. That's the point — progress you can feel in one session.

## Mark your three best

When you're done, mark your 3 best results with a star. These become the core of your prompt library — and later, your saved templates on Day 11 and your first automations on Day 17.

## Why this matters

Fluency isn't knowing the framework; it's using it until the prompts come out the way sentences do. Nobody gets fluent by reading about it. Ten tasks today, and the four parts stop being a framework and become instinct. That's how you walk into work on Monday and just use AI.

## 🎯 Task (15 minutes)

Run all 10 task types against your own work content: a real email you need to send, a real document, a real decision. Use the four-part framework from yesterday for each. When you finish, mark your 3 best results with a ⭐.

*Example: "Don't invent fake work. Use the report you're actually writing, the client email you're actually dreading, the meeting you actually sat through."*

## 📤 Output

Your **First Prompt Library**: all 10 prompts in your own words, with your 3 best marked. Save it somewhere permanent — notes app, document, wherever. Tomorrow: one real deliverable, produced in two different tools — the head-to-head that settles which tool earns your default slot.
