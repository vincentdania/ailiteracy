# Day 12 — Claude Deep Dive

**Read time: 5 minutes · Task: 15 minutes**

Until now, every chat has started from zero. Claude lets you bring your whole world into the conversation — your documents, your standards, your history.

## Projects: your knowledge base in the chat

**Claude Projects** is where this gets real. Create a project, upload your key documents — past reports, style guides, meeting notes — and every conversation inside that project draws on them. Ask *"based on our files, what did we commit to last quarter?"* and it answers from your own material, not general knowledge. It's the closest thing to a colleague who has read everything you've ever given them. Free tiers include Projects but cap how many messages you can use — so run focused sessions, not idle chats.

## Artifacts: documents that live and grow

**Artifacts** take the output to a new level. Instead of text in a chat box, Claude builds a document — a report, a memo, a table, even a small working tool — in a side panel, and you edit it together in real time. *"Shorten the intro"*, *"add a risk section"*, *"put the numbers in a table"* — the artifact updates in place. No more copy-pasting draft versions between apps. ChatGPT has a similar space called canvas; same idea, same habit: iterate in the living document, copy out when it's right.

## Long-context reading

Claude also reads long documents well. Upload a 50-page PDF or a full report and interrogate it:

- **"Summarise this in three paragraphs"**
- **"What are the risks in section four?"**
- **"List every deadline mentioned in this document"**

This is the research skill from Day 7, scaled up. Still verify: ask for the section reference and check it yourself.

## Why this matters

The difference between a tool and a teammate is context. Projects give Claude yours — your files, your standards, your history — so you stop chatting with a generic assistant and start working with one briefed on your job. The answers are only as good as the files you upload, so keep Projects fed, and keep sensitive documents out.

---

## 🎯 Task (15 minutes)

1. Create a **Claude Project** and upload 3–5 real work documents (redacted versions if anything is sensitive).
2. Ask **3 questions** against those documents: a summary, a specific fact (*"what did we agree about X?"*), and an action list.
3. In a new conversation, ask for **one artifact** — a one-page summary table of the key points.

*Example: "Based on the files in this project, summarise our client commitments and list every deadline."*

## 📤 Output

Your **project setup plus one artifact**. Screenshot the artifact and share it — it's your first piece of genuine Claude co-work.
