# Day 1 — What AI Can and Can't Do

**Read time: 5 minutes · Task: 10 minutes**

You've heard the hype. Let's start with the truth.

## AI is not magic

Underneath, tools like ChatGPT and Claude are prediction engines: they guess the next word in a sequence — billions of times, so well that the result looks like thinking. It isn't thinking. It's pattern matching at scale.

That sounds like a limitation, but it's exactly why these tools are useful. They've read more text than you ever will, and they can produce language, structure, and ideas faster than you can.

## Meet your new intern

The best mental model for working with AI is a brilliant, eager intern: fast, knowledgeable, always available — but new on the job.

Interns produce first drafts quickly. They also make confident mistakes. You wouldn't send an intern's work straight to your boss without checking it. Same with AI.

## What AI is genuinely great at

- **Drafting** — emails, reports, proposals: a 70% draft in seconds
- **Summarising** — long documents, meeting notes, research papers
- **Structuring** — outlines, plans, frameworks, checklists
- **Brainstorming** — ideas, angles, options you hadn't considered
- **Translating** — between languages, and between jargon and plain English

## What AI is unreliable at

- **Facts and figures** — it can state a wrong number with total confidence (hallucination)
- **Maths** — simple arithmetic trips it up; always verify
- **Judgement** — it has no stakes in your life; it won't know what your boss actually wants
- **Your private data** — unless you decide to share it, it stays yours

## The three rules of this challenge

1. **Verify everything.** Check numbers, dates, names. Ask for sources.
2. **Context matters.** AI is only as good as what you tell it. Feed it background, constraints, audience, tone.
3. **It learns from you.** Your edits, your follow-ups, your saved prompts — every interaction sharpens the output.

## Why this matters

The people who fail with AI treat it as a magic box. The people who win treat it as a fast intern they supervise. Today, you start becoming the supervisor.

## 🎯 Task (10 minutes)

List **5 tasks you do at work each week** that involve words, documents, or analysis. For each, rate:

- 🟢 **Great AI fit** — drafting, summarising, structuring, brainstorming
- 🟡 **Maybe** — needs your judgment but AI can help
- 🔴 **Keep human** — final decisions, sensitive info, anything with real consequences

*Example: "Weekly report — 🟢 · Signing off on a budget — 🔴 · Replying to client emails — 🟢"*

## 📤 Output

Your **AI Fit List**. This list becomes the map for the rest of the 21 days — Days 6–11 teach you exactly how to apply AI to your 🟢 items. Share it in the community if you like; keep it either way.
