# Day 17 — Agentic Tools You Can Use Today

**Read time: 5 minutes · Task: 15 minutes**

Yesterday you made the delegation list. Today you turn one item on it into something that actually runs — no code, no API, and no paid plan required.

## Three ways to make AI act

**1. ChatGPT Tasks.** If you use ChatGPT, look for Tasks in the interface: you describe a job and when it should run — "every weekday at 8am, draft a one-paragraph brief on today's tech news" — and it delivers on schedule. It's scheduled chat with a job description.

**2. Claude Projects.** You met Projects on Day 12: a knowledge base your AI always works from. Leave it on a repeating job — weekly report drafting, document summarising — and it becomes your quietest colleague.

**3. No-code flow tools.** Zapier, Make, and n8n all have free tiers. These connect your apps: new form response → draft reply; new spreadsheet row → summary to your email; new meeting → notes saved to your documents folder. You build the flow visually, by connecting boxes, not by programming.

## The safest first automations

- **Daily briefing** — news or data summary delivered each morning
- **Weekly report draft** — your week's numbers pulled into a first draft
- **Meeting follow-up** — after each call, generate a summary and action list
- **Reminders** — "check this invoice if it's still unpaid by Friday"

Each one has the same shape: it produces a draft or a nudge, and you do the final judgement. That's the sweet spot.

## The two things you don't automate

Money and secrets. No automation should move payments, hold your passwords, or touch confidential client data. If a flow needs a login, think twice; if it needs money, don't.

Automation is a habit, not a project. One flow today, checked daily, beats ten flows you built and forgot.

## Why this matters

A single working automation pays for this course in the time it saves. More importantly, it proves you can make AI act on a schedule — which is the difference between using AI and running on it.

## 🎯 Task (15 minutes)

Set up **one automation** from your Day 16 list — the easiest one. Any of these counts:

- A ChatGPT Task ("every weekday at 8am, brief me on…")
- A Claude Project set on a repeating job

Run it once manually to test, then leave it scheduled. If a tool asks for a credit card, stop — you don't need one.
