# AI for Your Work: commercial validation protocol

Status: NOT VALIDATED. This protocol is a prospective experiment, not evidence of demand. No invitations sent and no payments collected under this protocol.

## Initial market
Start with Nigerian programme and administrative professionals who regularly produce reports, meeting notes and correspondence. This is an initial hypothesis, not a proven best segment. Do not combine results across unrelated segments and claim universal demand.

## Offer to test
AI for Your Work: ten practical lessons, reusable prompts and a personal workflow for turning your own notes into checked work documents. Intended price: ₦20,000. No income, employment or guaranteed time-saving promises. Describe manual assistant workflows accurately; do not advertise autonomous software.

## Release prerequisites
Before accepting payment: course accessible, checkout verified, payment confirmation and enrolment verified, support contact present, accurate certificate rules, instructor recording available or its promise removed, refund policy approved and published. Current production deployment is unverified for this revised course. Do not recruit into an unavailable product as if it were ready.

## Stage 1: willingness to pay
Recruit 20 qualified prospects in the initial segment, including at least 10 without a close personal relationship to the instructor. Qualification: recurring document task, compatible phone/computer, willing to attempt the course and lawful non-confidential material available.

Present the same offer and full price. Record everyone approached, not only buyers. Separate referrals, personal contacts and independent buyers. Exclude complimentary seats, deposits, uncompleted checkout and instructor-funded purchases from paid conversion.

Provisional pass threshold: at least five independent completed full-price purchases from 20 qualified offers. This is an internal decision threshold, not an industry benchmark or statistically representative market estimate. Record objections even if the threshold passes.

## Stage 2: delivered value
Observe at least ten paying learners; recruit a second batch if needed. Record assistance rather than silently coaching every task. Review after Day 10 and again 14 days after completion.

Provisional internal thresholds:
- At least eight of ten produce a usable first output, judged against their source material.
- At least seven of ten complete the ten-day course and submissions.
- At least six of ten independently reuse a workflow after the course.
- At least seven of ten say the course was worth the actual price paid and explain why.
- No unresolved critical privacy, payment or access failure.

These small-sample thresholds support iteration in this segment; they do not validate five million buyers. Record refunds and requests in full, including reasons. Honour the approved policy regardless of targets.

## Stage 3: repeatable acquisition
Repeat with a fresh cohort not dependent on Vincent's personal persuasion. Log acquisition spending, payment fees, support time, refunds and other delivery costs. Commercial viability requires positive contribution after these costs, not merely gross sales. Do not assert a viable acquisition cost before observing it. Test additional segments separately.

## Baseline exercise
Before teaching the prompt, ask the learner to draft a short non-confidential report from a supplied fictional note set. Record elapsed time and errors. After instruction, use a different but comparable note set. Assess fidelity, missing actions, invented facts, clarity and required corrections. Distinguish practice effects from claims about general productivity.

## Interview questions
1. Show the last recurring document task you struggled with, with sensitive content removed.
2. What do you currently do, and what does it cost in time or money?
3. Which course output have you actually used?
4. What still required help?
5. What disappointed you at the price you paid?
6. Have you reused the workflow? Show an anonymised example if comfortable.
7. Would you recommend it? Why or why not? Do not ask for a testimonial as a condition of support.

## Draft invitation — review before sending
I am testing AI for Your Work, a ten-day practical course for people who regularly write reports and work documents. You will build reusable prompts and checked workflows using your own non-confidential tasks. The price is ₦20,000. This is a pilot: I will ask for frank feedback, including what does not work. It does not promise a job, income or autonomous software. Would you like the course details and the approved access/refund terms?

## Evidence and consent
Store minimal participant identifiers. Do not collect confidential work records. Obtain separate explicit consent before publishing any quote, screenshot or case study. Participation is not consent to marketing. Document all actual results, negative outcomes and limitations.

## Decisions
PASS: paid demand plus delivered-value criteria achieved; proceed to another cohort, not a mass-market success claim.
ITERATE: some paid demand but weak completion or repeat use; fix content/onboarding and rerun.
REPOSITION: useful outputs but weak full-price purchases; investigate segment, offer and pricing without rewriting evidence.
STOP: repeated weak demand or material unresolved harms.

## Public claim after a passing pilot
Only use a claim populated from actual records, such as: tested with paying learners in a named segment; report exact sample size, actual price, observed completion and follow-up use. Never claim nationwide validation from a convenience sample.
